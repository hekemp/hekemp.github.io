Panang Curry

.cook
👥 6 servings 🌍 Thai 📖 https://hot-thai-kitchen.com/panang-curry/

🥘 Ingredients

  • bell peppers (cut into strips)
    2
  • chicken breast (sliced)
    1.3 lbs
  • coconut cream (Coco Reàl preferred)
  • coconut milk (Aroy-D carton preferred, pouring at a slight angle to get the creamier part)
    2.5 cup
  • fish sauce
    4.5 tbsp
  • jasmine rice
  • lime juice (Optional)
    1 tbsp
  • makrut/kaffir lime Leaves (about 2 whole dried makrut lime leaves)
    1.5 tsp
  • onion (diced)
    1
  • palm sugar (chopped)
    110g
  • panang curry paste (Maesri preferred)
    1 4 oz can
  • peanut butter
    2 tbsp
  • thai sweet basil (Optional) (specifically Thai sweet basil, not holy basil or other types)
    1 tbsp

🍳 Cookware

  • wok
  1. 1
    Remember to start your jasmine rice before beginning.
    jasmine rice
  2. 📝

    If you are confident in your cooking ability, you can caramelize palm sugar here, but it is very easy to burn things as it requires high heat. For this simplified recipe, we omit that step here.

  3. 2
    Add coconut milk to a wok and heat on medium-high heat until thick. Your goal is to lightly caramelize the milk.
    coconut milk: 1.25 cup (Aroy-D carton preferred, pouring at a slight angle to get the creamier part)
  4. 📝

    If you're using canned unsweetened coconut milk, use two 14oz cans. Do not shake them. For this step, simply skim the top half of each can for the creamier part.

  5. 3
    Add panang curry paste and coconut cream and fry off a bit.
    panang curry paste: 1 4 oz can (Maesri preferred), coconut cream (Coco Reàl preferred)
  6. 4
    Add the remaining coconut milk and palm sugar . Mix to combine.
    coconut milk: 1.25 cup, palm sugar: 110g (chopped)
  7. 5
    Add fish sauce , peanut butter , and lime juice . Mix to combine.
    fish sauce: 4.5 tbsp, peanut butter: 2 tbsp, lime juice: 1 tbsp (Optional)
  8. 📝

    You can replace the fish sauce in a pinch or to avoid fish allergies by using 3 tbsp soy sauce and 1.5 tbsp rice vinegar.

  9. 6
    Add bell peppers , onion , and chicken breast . Mix to combine.
    bell peppers: 2 (cut into strips), onion: 1 (diced), chicken breast: 1.3 lbs (sliced)
  10. 7
    Cover and simmer until cooked, about ⏱️ 15 minutes total. At the 10 minute mark, add thai sweet basil and makrut/kaffir lime Leaves .
    thai sweet basil: 1 tbsp (Optional) (specifically Thai sweet basil, not holy basil or other types), makrut/kaffir lime Leaves: 1.5 tsp (about 2 whole dried makrut lime leaves)
  11. 📝

    Be careful to not overcook the lime leaves, as over cooking them removes the citrus flavor. Undercooking, of course, misses out on this flavor.

  12. 8
    Serve on top of your jasmine rice.
  13. 📝

    If you prefer a thicker panang, make a slurry of 1-2 tbsp cornstarch and mix with a little cold water. Add this to your panang curry.

  14. 📝
