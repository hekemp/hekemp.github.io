Pad Thai

.cook
👥 3 servings 🌍 Thai 📖 https://hot-thai-kitchen.com/best-pad-thai/

🥘 Ingredients

  • bean sprouts (Optional)
    2.5 cups
  • chicken breast (chopped)
    1 lbs
  • chili flakes (to taste)
  • dry rice noodles
    240-300g
  • eggs
    2
  • garlic (chopped)
    9 cloves
  • garlic chives (Optional) (cut into 2-inch pieces)
    70g
  • lime wedge (Optional)
  • oil
    3 tbsp
  • onion (diced)
    1
  • pad thai sauce (Mae Ploy brand is recommended)
    9 tbsp
  • peanut butter
    1 tbsp
  • pepper (Optional)
  • roasted peanuts (Optional) (roughly crushed, roughly crushed)
    1/4 cup
  • salt (Optional)
  • water

🍳 Cookware

  • pot
  • wok or large nonstick skillet
  1. 1
    Boil water in a pot and then turn off heat. Add dry rice noodles and let soak in warm water until cooked, about ⏱️ 5 minutes .
    water, dry rice noodles: 240-300g
  2. 2
    Strain noodles and set aside.
  3. 3
    Heat a wok or large nonstick skillet over high heat and add just enough oil to coat the bottom. Sear chicken breast until done and remove from pan. Season your chicken with chili flakes , salt , pepper , garlic , etc. as desired here.
    oil: 1 tbsp, chicken breast: 1 lbs (chopped), chili flakes (Optional), salt (Optional), pepper (Optional), garlic (Optional)
  4. 4
    In the same wok, now over medium heat, add oil , and then add garlic , chili flakes , and onion . Sauté for a few minutes, until the garlic starts to turn golden and the onion is translucent. If the wok looks dry, add a little more oil.
    oil: 1 tbsp, garlic: 9 cloves (chopped), chili flakes (to taste), onion: 1 (diced)
  5. 5
    Turn heat up to high and then add peanut butter and pad thai sauce . Mix, ensuring to keep from burning with water or oil.
    peanut butter: 1 tbsp, pad thai sauce: 4.5 tbsp (Mae Ploy brand is recommended)
  6. 6
    Add noodles and additional pad thai sauce . Keep tossing until all the sauce is absorbed.
    pad thai sauce: 4.5 tbsp
  7. 7
    Once sauce is absorbed, you can turn off the heat and taste the noodles for doneness. If they're still undercooked, add a little more water and continue cooking.
  8. 8
    Once noodles are done, turn heat back to medium and push them to one side of the pan. Add a little extra oil to the empty space and add eggs .
    oil: 1 tbsp, eggs: 2
  9. 9
    Break the yolks, then put noodles on top of eggs and cook for about 30 seconds. Flip and toss to mix eggs into noodles.
  10. 10
    Toss the cooked protein back in, plus any collected juices.
  11. 11
    Add bean sprouts , garlic chives , and roasted peanuts .
    bean sprouts: 2.5 cups (Optional), garlic chives: 70g (Optional) (cut into 2-inch pieces), roasted peanuts: 1/8 cup (Optional) (roughly crushed)
  12. 12
    Turn off the heat and toss until well mixed.
  13. 13
    Serve immediately with a lime wedge and roasted peanuts on top.
    lime wedge (Optional), roasted peanuts: 1/8 cup (Optional) (roughly crushed)
