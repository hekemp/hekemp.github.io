Cinnamon Rolls

.cook
👥 8 servings ⏱️ Prep Time: 30 minutes 🔥 Cook Time: 18 minutes 👤 Charlotte Rutledge 📖 https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/perfectly-pillowy-cinnamon-rolls-recipe

🥘 Ingredients

Tangzhong

  • bread flour
    3 tbsp
  • milk
    1/2 cup

Dough

  • bread flour
    2.5 cups
  • butter (unsalted, softened)
    4 tbsp
  • instant yeast
    2 tsp
  • milk (cold)
    2/3 cup
  • salt
    1 tsp
  • sugar
    2 tbsp

Filling

  • bread flour
    2 tbsp
  • brown sugar (light, packed)
    1/2 cup
  • butter (melted)
    1 tbsp
  • cinnamon
    4 tsp
  • salt
    0.063 tsp

Baking

  • butter (melted)
    1.5 tbsp

Icing

  • butter (melted)
    1.5 tbsp
  • confectioners' sugar (sifted)
    1.5 cups
  • milk (sub heavy cream or buttermilk, enough to thin to desired consistency but no more than 2 tbsp)
    1 tbsp
  • salt
    0.063 tsp
  • vanilla extract
    1/2 tsp

🍳 Cookware

  • small saucepan
  • stand mixer
  • medium bowl
  • baking sheet
  • medium bowl

Tangzhong

  1. 1
    Combine milk and bread flour in a small saucepan and whisk until no lumps remain.
    milk: 1/2 cup, bread flour: 3 tbsp
  2. 2
    Place the saucepan over medium heat and cook the mixture, stirring regularly, until thickened, paste-like, and the spoon or spatula leaves lines on the bottom of the pan. This should take about ⏱️ 1-3 minutes , depending on the strength of your burner.
  3. 3
    Remove from the heat and transfer to the bowl of a stand mixer .

Dough

  1. 1
    Add milk , bread flour , salt , sugar , instant yeast , and butter to the mixing bowl. The heat from the tangzhong will help to warm the cold milk, so ensure you add that first.
    milk: 2/3 cup (cold), bread flour: 2.5 cups, salt: 1 tsp, sugar: 2 tbsp, instant yeast: 2 tsp, butter: 4 tbsp (unsalted, softened)
  2. 2
    Mix on low speed with the dough hook attachment to bring the dough together.
  3. 3
    Knead the dough until it's smooth, elastic, and tacky. This will take ⏱️ 10-12 minutes on medium-low speed of a mixer.
  4. 4
    Shape the dough into a ball, place it in a bowl, and cover the bowl with plastic wrap or a reusable cover.
  5. 5
    Let the dough rise until puffy but not necessarily doubled in bulk, about ⏱️ 60-90 minutes , depending on the warmth of your kitchen.

Filling

  1. 1
    Put butter into a medium bowl and add brown sugar , bread flour , cinnamon , and salt . Stir until the mixture is the texture of damp sand. Set aside.
    butter: 1 tbsp (melted), brown sugar: 1/2 cup (light, packed), bread flour: 2 tbsp, cinnamon: 4 tsp, salt: 0.063 tsp

Assembly

  1. 1
    Lightly grease a baking sheet or line it with parchment paper.
  2. 2
    Transfer the dough to a lightly greased work surface and press it into a 10” x 12” rectangle that's about 1/2” thick. For evenly shaped cinnamon rolls, try to pat the dough into an actual rectangle (with corners), rather than an oval.
  3. 3
    Sprinkle the filling over the dough, covering all but a 1/2” strip along one long side.
  4. 4
    Starting with the filling-covered long side, roll the dough into a log.
  5. 5
    Score the dough lightly into eight equal 1 1/2” to 2” pieces; this will make large, saucer-sized cinnamon rolls. Cut the dough at the score marks.
  6. 📝

    Dental floss will give you the cleanest cut: pull off a long piece of floss, loop it underneath the log at the score mark, and pull the ends in opposite directions to cut the dough. Repeat until you've cut all of the cinnamon rolls. If you don't have dental floss, a bench knife or sharp knife will work.

  7. 6
    Place the cinnamon rolls onto the prepared baking sheet, spacing them so there's at least 2” between each one and they're 2” away from the edges of the pan; a 3-2-3 arrangement works well. To prevent them from unraveling while they rise and bake, tuck the ends of the spirals underneath the cinnamon rolls so that they're held in place.
  8. 7
    Cover the cinnamon rolls with lightly greased plastic wrap or a reusable cover and let them rise for about ⏱️ 30-60 minutes (depending on the warmth of your kitchen). The cinnamon rolls should be puffy and the dough shouldn't bounce back immediately when gently pressed.

Baking

  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 375°F.
  2. 2
    Bake the cinnamon rolls for about ⏱️ 14-18 minutes , until they're a light golden brown and a digital thermometer inserted into the center of one roll reads 190°F. Bake for the lesser amount of time for extra-soft cinnamon rolls, and the longer amount of time for cinnamon rolls with a bit more color and slightly firmer texture.
  3. 3
    Remove the cinnamon rolls from the oven, place the pan on a rack, and brush the hot cinnamon rolls with butter . Let the cinnamon rolls cool for about ⏱️ 10-15 minutes before icing.
    butter: 1.5 tbsp (melted)

Icing

  1. 1
    Combine butter , confectioners' sugar , milk , vanilla extract , and salt in a medium bowl , mixing with a spatula until smooth.
    butter: 1.5 tbsp (melted), confectioners' sugar: 1.5 cups (sifted), milk: 1 tbsp (sub heavy cream or buttermilk, enough to thin to desired consistency but no more than 2 tbsp), vanilla extract: 1/2 tsp, salt: 0.063 tsp
  2. 📝

    As for which option for milk, milk makes more of a frosting, heavy cream creates an extra layer of richness, while buttermilk adds subtle tang, a nice counterpoint to the icing's overall sweetness. You can also go for cream cheese instead of vanilla frosting by using 4-6 tbsp of softened cream cheese

  3. 2
    Ice the cinnamon rolls and serve immediately. If you're planning to serve the cinnamon rolls later, wait to ice them until just before serving. Store icing at room temperature, tightly covered, until you're ready to use it.
  4. 📝

    Store completely cooled cinnamon rolls, un-iced and well wrapped, for a couple of days at room temperature; or freeze for up to 1 month.
