Apple Cider Donuts

.cook
👥 18 servings 📖 https://smittenkitchen.com/2009/10/apple-cider-donuts/

🥘 Ingredients

Donuts

  • apple cider
    1 cup
  • baking powder
    2 teaspoons
  • baking soda
    1 teaspoon
  • butter (at room temperature)
    4 tablespoons
  • buttermilk
    1/2 cup
  • eggs
    2 large
  • flour
    3.5 cups
  • granulated sugar
    1 cup
  • ground cinnamon
    1/2 teaspoon
  • ground nutmeg
    1/8 teaspoon
  • salt
    1/2 teaspoon
  • vegetable oil or shortening

Toppings

  • apple cider
    2 tablespoons
  • confectioners' sugar
    1 cup
  • granulated sugar
    1 cup
  • ground cinnamon
    1.5 tablespoons

🍳 Cookware

  • saucepan
  • bowl
  • standing mixer
  • baking sheets
  • donut cutter
  • deep fryer

Donuts

  1. 1
    In a saucepan over medium or medium-low heat, gently reduce apple cider to about 1/4 cup, ⏱️ 20-30 minutes . Set aside to cool.
    apple cider: 1 cup
  2. 2
    Meanwhile, in a bowl , combine flour , baking powder , baking soda , ground cinnamon , salt , and ground nutmeg . Set aside.
    flour: 3.5 cups, baking powder: 2 teaspoons, baking soda: 1 teaspoon, ground cinnamon: 1/2 teaspoon, salt: 1/2 teaspoon, ground nutmeg: 1/8 teaspoon
  3. 3
    Using a standing mixer on medium speed with the paddle attachment, beat butter and granulated sugar until the mixture is smooth. Add eggs , one at a time, and continue to beat until the eggs are completely incorporated. Use a spatula to scrape down the sides of the bowl occasionally.
    butter: 4 tablespoons (at room temperature), granulated sugar: 1 cup, eggs: 2 large
  4. 4
    Reduce the speed to low and gradually add the reduced apple cider and buttermilk , mixing just until combined. Add the flour mixture and continue to mix just until the dough comes together.
    buttermilk: 1/2 cup
  5. 5
    Line two baking sheets with parchment or wax paper and sprinkle them generously with flour. Turn the dough onto one of the sheets and sprinkle the top with flour. Flatten the dough with your hands until it is about 1/2 inch thick. Use more flour if the dough is still wet. Transfer the dough to the freezer until it is slightly hardened, about ⏱️ 20 minutes .
  6. 6
    Pull the dough out of the freezer. Using a donut cutter (3-inch or 3.5-inch), cut out donut shapes. Place the cut donuts and donut holes onto the second sheet pan. Refrigerate the donuts for ⏱️ 20-30 minutes .
  7. 7
    Add vegetable oil or shortening to your deep fryer and heat the oil to 350°F.
    vegetable oil or shortening
  8. 8
    Carefully add a few donuts to the oil, being careful not to crowd the pan, and fry until golden brown, about ⏱️ 60 seconds . Turn the donuts over and fry until the other side is golden, ⏱️ 30 seconds to ⏱️ 60 seconds . Drain on paper towels for a minute after the donuts are fried.

Toppings

  1. 1
    Whisk together confectioners' sugar and apple cider until smooth. Make cinnamon sugar by mixing granulated sugar and ground cinnamon together. Set aside.
    confectioners' sugar: 1 cup, apple cider: 2 tablespoons, granulated sugar: 1 cup, ground cinnamon: 1.5 tablespoons
  2. 2
    Dip the top of the warm donuts into the glaze or cinnamon sugar mixture (if using) and serve immediately.
