2 Ingredient Donut Holes

.cook
👥 8 servings 📖 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vzKuobmi3iI

🥘 Ingredients

Donuts

  • baking powder (alternatively, use .5 tsp baking soda)
    1.5 tsp
  • cinnamon (Optional)
    1 tbsp
  • flour
    1 cup
  • oil
  • yogurt (plain, can substitute with 112g/half cup of milk)
    190 g

Toppings

  • butter (melted)
    2/3 stick
  • melted chocolate or cocoa powder (Optional) (to taste)
  • milk (add another tbsp if you like thin glaze)
    3 tbsp
  • powdered sugar
    2 cups
  • sugar (Optional)
  • vanilla
    1 tsp

🍳 Cookware

  • pan

Donuts

  1. 1
    Create self-rising flour by combining flour with baking powder . Combine with yogurt . For a little more flavor add cinnamon .
    flour: 1 cup, baking powder: 1.5 tsp (alternatively, use .5 tsp baking soda), yogurt: 190 g (plain, can substitute with 112g/half cup of milk), cinnamon: 1 tbsp (Optional)
  2. 2
    Preheat oil in your pan to around 325°F.
    oil
  3. 3
    Put dough in a ziplock bag and cut the corner to create a pipet. Squeeze a little dough out and then slice with your scissors to create easy donut holes. Due to no yeast, they will drop to the bottom at first, but after a while they should float. Use a slotted spoon or spatula to move them around constantly, until they are golden. Then remove from oil and let rest on a paper towel.

Toppings

  1. 1
    For simple sugar coated donuts, simply toss in sugar .
    sugar (Optional)
  2. 2
    For a glaze, mix powdered sugar , butter , vanilla , and milk . To make this a chocolate glaze, add melted chocolate or cocoa powder .
    powdered sugar: 2 cups, butter: 2/3 stick (melted), vanilla: 1 tsp, milk: 3 tbsp (add another tbsp if you like thin glaze), melted chocolate or cocoa powder (Optional) (to taste)
