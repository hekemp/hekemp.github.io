Buttermilk Donuts

.cook
#buttermilk #donuts

This no-yeast buttermilk donut is easy to make and ready in minutes to feed a hungry crowd!

👥 24 servings 🍽️ bread, Breakfast, Dessert, donuts ⏱️ Prep Time: 10 minutes 🔥 Cook Time: 20 minutes 🌍 American 📖 https://abountifulkitchen.com/homemade-buttermilk-donuts/

🥘 Ingredients

  • baking powder
    1 teaspoon
  • baking soda
    1 teaspoon
  • butter (melted)
    6 tablespoons
  • buttermilk (room temperature, can substitute with 1 cup milk and 1 cup plain Greek yogurt)
    2 cups
  • cinnamon
    1 teaspoon
  • eggs (beaten, room temperature)
    2 large
  • flour
    6 cups
  • nutmeg
    1/2 teaspoon
  • oil
  • salt
    1 teaspoon
  • sugar
    2 cups

🍳 Cookware

  • stand mixer
  • donut cutter
  • deep fryer
  1. 1
    In the bowl of a stand mixer with the paddle attachment, combine buttermilk , eggs and sugar . Mix until sugar is incorporated into buttermilk and eggs.
    buttermilk: 2 cups (room temperature, can substitute with 1 cup milk and 1 cup plain Greek yogurt), eggs: 2 large (beaten, room temperature), sugar: 1.25 cups
  2. 2
    Add baking soda , baking powder , salt , cinnamon , nutmeg , and butter . Mix again until incorporated with buttermilk mixture.
    baking soda: 1 teaspoon, baking powder: 1 teaspoon, salt: 1 teaspoon, cinnamon: 1 teaspoon, nutmeg: 1/2 teaspoon, butter: 6 tablespoons (melted)
  3. 3
    Add flour and mix on low until the flour is no longer visible. Mix in an additional flour , a half cup at a time, scraping down sides and bottom of bowl.
    flour: 4 cups, flour: 2 cups
  4. 4
    Mix in additional flour if needed. The dough should take shape and not have a wet look. If the dough is still wet or sticky, add an additional tablespoon or two of flour . Turn the dough in the bowl until the dough is no longer sticky.
    flour
  5. 5
    Generously flour a pastry mat or clean surface. Turn the dough onto the surface and knead 3-4 times, folding the dough onto itself and gathering flour to make dough less sticky if needed. Roll the dough out to about 1/4-1/2 inch thick.
  6. 6
    Using a donut cutter , cut the donut shapes out.
  7. 7
    Heat oil in your deep fryer to 375°F. Let cook on one side until golden, then turn with chopsticks to cook the other side. It takes about ⏱️ 1-2 minutes per side to cook. Drain donuts on a paper towel lined cookie sheet. Cook the donut holes in 2-3 batches.
    oil
  8. 8
    Let cool for about a minute.
  9. 9
    Place sugar into a paper bag. Toss the donuts 3-4 at a time in the bag of sugar.
    sugar: 3/4 cups
  10. 10
    Serve warm.
