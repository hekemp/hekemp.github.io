Cinnamon-Sugar Crunch Bagels

.cook

Going to Panera's for their Cinnamon Crunch Bagels is expensive, and finding good sugary bagels is hard these days...

👥 6 servings ⏱️ Prep Time: 45 minutes 🔥 Cook Time: 22 minutes 👤 Molly Marzalek-Kelly 📖 https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/cinnamon-sugar-crunch-bagels-recipe

🥘 Ingredients

Preferment (pâte fermentée)

  • bread flour (about 1 cup)
    120 g
  • instant yeast
    0.063 teaspoon
  • salt
    1/8 teaspoon
  • water (or 75g, cooled)
    1/3 cup

Dough

  • bread flour (about 2.5 cups)
    300 g
  • cinnamon (Vietnamese/Saigon preferred)
    1/4 tsp
  • cinnamon sweet bits (Optional)
    1/4 cup
  • instant yeast
    3/4 teaspoon
  • light brown sugar (13g, packed)
    1 tbsp
  • salt
    1.25 teaspoons
  • vanilla extract
    1 teaspoon
  • water (about 3/4 cup, warm)
    170 g

Creating the Topping

  • bread flour
    2 tablespoons
  • butter (melted)
    1 tbsp
  • coarse sparkling sugar
    1 tbsp
  • granulated sugar
    1 tablespoon
  • ground cinnamon (Vietnamese/Saigon preferred)
    3/4 teaspoon
  • light brown sugar (packed)
    2 tablespoons
  • vanilla extract
    1 teaspoon

Cooking the Bagels

  • Cinnamon Sweet Bits (Optional)
  • coarse sparkling sugar (Optional)
  • egg (beaten)
    1
  • honey
    2 tbsp
  • water
    8 cups, 1 tbsp

🍳 Cookware

  • bowl
  • stand mixer
  • baking sheet
  • bowl
  • pot
  • bowl

Preferment (pâte fermentée)

  1. 1
    In a bowl , combine bread flour , salt , instant yeast , and water to make a shaggy dough. Use your hands to knead gently until thoroughly combined. This may take about a minute; it's quite stiff.
    bread flour: 120 g (about 1 cup), salt: 1/8 teaspoon, instant yeast: 0.063 teaspoon, water: 1/3 cup (or 75g, cooled)
  2. 📝

    While this recipe calls for instant yeast, you can use an equal amount of active dry yeast. You probably want to activate it with warm water and a splash of sugar first before combining with the salt though, to ensure they don't kill the yeast.

  3. 2
    Cover the bowl and let the preferment rest at room temperature for ⏱️ 4-14 hours ; it should expand.

Dough

  1. 1
    To the bowl with the preferment, add water , vanilla extract , light brown sugar , instant yeast , salt , cinnamon , and bread flour .
    water: 170 g (about 3/4 cup, warm), vanilla extract: 1 teaspoon, light brown sugar: 1 tbsp (13g, packed), instant yeast: 3/4 teaspoon, salt: 1.25 teaspoons, cinnamon: 1/4 tsp (Vietnamese/Saigon preferred), bread flour: 300 g (about 2.5 cups)
  2. 2
    Mix at low speed in a stand mixer with the dough hook attachment to bring the dough together.
  3. 3
    Next, knead the stiff dough until it's supple and elastic. This will take about ⏱️ 8-10 minutes at medium-low speed with a mixer. During the last 2 minutes of kneading, add cinnamon sweet bits if using.
    cinnamon sweet bits: 1/4 cup (Optional)
  4. 4
    Lift the dough out of the stand mixer bowl so you can lightly grease it with oil, then return dough to the bowl and let it rest, covered, until it doubles in bulk. This will take about ⏱️ 2-2.5 hours depending on the warmth of your kitchen.

Preshaping the Dough

  1. 1
    Turn the dough out onto a clean, lightly floured surface.
  2. 2
    Gently deflate it with your hands, then divide it into 6 equal pieces (about 120g each).
  3. 3
    One piece of dough at a time, fold the edges into the center until you can pinch to seal. You should be making essentially dough dumplings.
  4. 4
    Clear the flour from your work surface, if any remain. Then, starting with the first piece of dough you shaped, place it seam-side down. Use your cupped fingers and palm to roll the dough into a tight ball, moving your hand in a circular motion.
  5. 📝

    The dough should stick slightly to the surface in order to create tension to yield a smooth exterior. If the dough is unmanageably sticky, add a very light dusting of flour; if the dough slides around on the surface without tightening, use a damp towel to clean and moisten the surface and try again.

  6. 5
    Cover the preshaped dough and allow it to rest for ⏱️ 10-15 minutes .

Shaping the Bagels

  1. 1
    Line a baking sheet with parchment and lightly grease the parchment paper.
  2. 2
    Use your finger to poke a hole through the center of a dough ball. Gently rotate the dough to expand the hole to 2" to 3" in diameter, forming a ring shape. Place the shaped bagel onto the prepared baking sheet. The hole will close up a bit; that's OK.
  3. 3
    Cover the bagels again and let them rest until they feel slightly puffy to the touch, about ⏱️ 30 minutes .

Creating the Topping

  1. 1
    In a bowl , whisk light brown sugar , granulated sugar , coarse sparkling sugar , bread flour , and ground cinnamon together.
    light brown sugar: 2 tablespoons (packed), granulated sugar: 1 tablespoon, coarse sparkling sugar: 1 tbsp, bread flour: 2 tablespoons, ground cinnamon: 3/4 teaspoon (Vietnamese/Saigon preferred)
  2. 2
    Add vanilla extract and butter and stir until the mixture resembles wet sand.
    vanilla extract: 1 teaspoon, butter: 1 tbsp (melted)

Cooking the Bagels

  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 450°F.
  2. 2
    Combine water and honey in a wide, shallow pot ; the water should be at least 1.25" deep.
    water: 8 cups, honey: 2 tbsp
  3. 3
    Bring the mixture to a boil.
  4. 4
    Add bagels to the water two or three at a time to avoid crowding. Boil for ⏱️ 60 seconds , then use a dough whisk or slotted spoon to turn the bagels over.
  5. 5
    Boil for another ⏱️ 60 seconds before removing from the water and placing back on the prepared pan.
  6. 📝

    If the pan is sticky when you remove the bagels, just prep a new one, it's not worth the hassle.

  7. 6
    In a bowl , combine egg and water .
    egg: 1 (beaten), water: 1 tbsp
  8. 7
    Brush each bagel with the egg wash, then sprinkle with the topping, distributing evenly across the six bagels. For even more cinnamon crunch, sprinkle the tops of the bagels with more Cinnamon Sweet Bits and/or coarse sparkling sugar . Press down on the toppings to fully adhere them to the bagel, ensuring that everything stays put.
    Cinnamon Sweet Bits (Optional), coarse sparkling sugar (Optional)
  9. 8
    Bake the cinnamon bagels for ⏱️ 18-22 minutes , until golden brown. Rotate the pan (front to back, back to front) halfway through baking to ensure even browning. If at any point the topping is becoming too dark, tent with foil and continue baking.
  10. 9
    Remove the cinnamon bagels from the oven and transfer them to a rack to cool.
  11. 📝

    Store any leftover bagels in a paper bag for up to 2 days; freezing is not recommended, as the sugary topping will become sticky upon thawing. To re-crisp, bagels can be toasted or wrapped loosely in foil and placed in a 350°F oven until warm.

Cinnamon Raisin Variant

  1. 1
    Substitute 1/4 cup (43g) raisins for the Cinnamon Sweet Bits in the dough. Soften the raisins by soaking them in hot water for 15 minutes before kneading into the dough. Bake as directed.
