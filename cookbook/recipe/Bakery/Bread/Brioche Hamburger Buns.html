Brioche Hamburger Bun

.cook
#baking #Bread #Buns #Burgers
🍽️ lunch, Main Course ⏱️ Prep Time: 2 hours 45 minutes 🔥 Cook Time: 15 minutes 🌍 American 📖 https://blog.thermoworks.com/homemade-hamburger-bun-recipe/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=RFXPmax&utm_term=_

🥘 Ingredients

Make the dough

  • bread flour (about 3.5 cup)
    500 g
  • dry instant yeast (about 1 1/8 tbsp)
    10 g
  • granulated sugar (about 3 1/3 tbsp)
    40 g
  • milk
    2/3 cup
  • sea salt (about 2 tsp)
    10 g
  • unsalted butter (cold, 1/4 inch cubes, about 3.5tbsp)
    50 g
  • water (at appropriate temp)
    2/3 cup

Form the buns

  • egg yolk
    1
  • eggs (whole)
    2

Bake the buns

  • sesame seeds ()

🍳 Cookware

  • stand mixer
  • parchment-lined sheet tray

Make the dough

  1. 1
    First, determine the water temperature you need. Subtract the temperature of your milk from 150°F. That is the temperature your water must be water .
    milk: 2/3 cup, water: 2/3 cup (at appropriate temp)
  2. 2
    Combine the water and milk. The resulting mix should be 75°F (24°C).
  3. 3
    In a stand mixer with a dough hook, combine water/milk, bread flour , sea salt , granulated sugar , dry instant yeast and unsalted butter on low-speed for ⏱️ 5 minutes .
    bread flour: 500 g (about 3.5 cup), sea salt: 10 g (about 2 tsp), granulated sugar: 40 g (about 3 1/3 tbsp), dry instant yeast: 10 g (about 1 1/8 tbsp), unsalted butter: 50 g (cold, 1/4 inch cubes, about 3.5tbsp)
  4. 4
    Increase the speed of the mixer to medium and mix for ⏱️ 12-15 minutes . It's important to knead until the dough forms a gluten window.
  5. 5
    Turn the dough out onto a floured surface and make a smooth ball out of it.
  6. 6
    Put it back into a bowl, flour the surface lightly, cover it and let it ferment on the countertop for ⏱️ 60 minutes .

Form the buns

  1. 1
    Turn the dough back out of the bowl onto the floured surface and deflate it gently.
  2. 2
    Using a scale, divide the dough into 12 pieces, about 78g each. Don't flour them as you cut them.
  3. 3
    Make balls out of each by rolling each piece of dough on an un-floured section of the counter under a cupped hand until you feel it come together in a tight, sphere-ish shape.
  4. 4
    Place them on a parchment-lined sheet tray .
  5. 5
    Cover the dough balls with another sheet of parchment, and squash them down with another sheet tray until they are somewhat flattened.
  6. 6
    Make egg wash by vigorously beating eggs and egg yolk .
    eggs: 2 (whole), egg yolk: 1
  7. 7
    Baste the buns with the egg wash.
  8. 8
    Put them in an area where they can proof at around 75-80°F. Proof for about ⏱️ 90 minutes or until doubled in volume.
  9. 📝

    If you live in a very dry climate or your proof box is very dry, you may want to very lightly drape some plastic wrap over the buns to prevent them from drying out.

Bake the buns

  1. 1
    Remove the buns from the proofer and preheat your oven to 350°F.
  2. 2
    Very gently give the buns another coating of egg wash, then sprinkle them with sesame seeds .
    sesame seeds ()
  3. 3
    Bake the buns for ⏱️ 15-20 minutes , or until the inside reads no lower than 180–190°F.
  4. 📝

    A higher temp may occur if you want better browning on the tops of the buns, but anything below 200°F will still be great.

  5. 4
    Let the buns cool on a rack until ready to slice and use.
