Garlic Naan

.cook
📖 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uLt663PbRfM

🥘 Ingredients

Dough

  • active dry yeast
    2 tsp
  • all-purpose flour
    3 cups, 2 tbsp
  • oil (sub ghee)
  • olive oil
    1/4 cup
  • plain yoghurt (whole milk preferred)
    1/3 cup
  • salt
    1.5 tsp
  • sugar
    1 tsp
  • water (warm, warm)
    2/3 cup, 2 tbsp

Garlic Butter

  • cilantro (chopped)
    1 tbsp
  • garlic (minced)
    2 tsp
  • salt
    1/4 tsp
  • unsalted butter (melted)
    1/4 cup

🍳 Cookware

  • small bowl
  • stand mixer
  • skillet
  • small bowl

Dough

  1. 1
    Proof your yeast by adding active dry yeast to sugar and water in a small bowl .
    active dry yeast: 2 tsp, sugar: 1 tsp, water: 2 tbsp (warm)
  2. 2
    Whisk yeast with a fork, then cover to let rest for ⏱️ 10 minutes . The yeast should now be fluffy and bubbly.
  3. 3
    In a stand mixer with the dough hook attachment, mix all-purpose flour , plain yoghurt , salt , olive oil , water , and the proofed yeast.
    all-purpose flour: 3 cups, plain yoghurt: 1/3 cup (whole milk preferred), salt: 1.5 tsp, olive oil: 1/4 cup, water: 2/3 cup (warm)
  4. 4
    Ensure the dough is elastic but doesn't stick to your hands. If it does, add all-purpose flour until the dough no longer sticks to your hands.
    all-purpose flour: 2 tbsp (Optional)
  5. 5
    Move the rounded dough ball to a lightly oiled bowl. Cover and let rise for about ⏱️ 1 hour , or until doubled in size.
  6. 6
    Divide the dough into seven palm sized balls.
  7. 7
    Oil a plate generously and place a dough ball on the plate. Slowly stretch out the dough, working from the inside out. When done, grease the top of your dough.
  8. 8
    Heat skillet very hot - it will probably be steaming or smoking. Add some oil and then add your naan. If your pan is hot enough bubbles will start to form in the naan.
    oil (sub ghee)
  9. 9
    Cook both sides of naan until golden brown.

Garlic Butter

  1. 1
    In a small bowl mix unsalted butter , garlic , salt , and cilantro . Brush onto all sides of the cooked naan.
    unsalted butter: 1/4 cup (melted), garlic: 2 tsp (minced), salt: 1/4 tsp, cilantro: 1 tbsp (chopped)
