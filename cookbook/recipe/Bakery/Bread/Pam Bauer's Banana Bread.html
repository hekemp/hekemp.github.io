Pam Bauer's Banana Bread

.cook
#midwest

My old neighbor's banana bread recipe she'd shared with us

🥘 Ingredients

  • baking soda
    1 tsp
  • bananas (ripe, mashed)
    3
  • brown sugar
    1/2 cup
  • butter (melted)
    1/2 cup
  • eggs
    2
  • flour
    2 cups
  • milk
    4 tbsp
  • nuts (Optional) (walnut preferred, chopped)
    1/2 cup
  • sugar
    1 cup
  • vanilla
    1 tsp
  • vinegar (realistically just a few drops)
    0.167 tsp

🍳 Cookware

  • large loaf pan
  • small loaf pan
  1. 1
    Sour milk by adding vinegar to milk . Stir to combine then let sit for ⏱️ 5 minutes .
    vinegar: 0.167 tsp (realistically just a few drops), milk: 4 tbsp
  2. 📝

    To create sour milk, the ratio is 1 tbsp white vinegar to 1 cup of milk, so we need approximately 1/16 tbsp here, or just a smidgen more than 1/6 tsp. A few drops is honestly easier.

  3. 2
    If using nuts, toast nuts at 350°F on an ungreased baking pan for ⏱️ 6-8 minutes .
    nuts: 1/2 cup (Optional) (walnut preferred, chopped)
  4. 3
    Preheat oven to 325°F.
  5. 4
    Cream together sugar , brown sugar , and eggs .
    sugar: 1 cup, brown sugar: 1/2 cup, eggs: 2
  6. 5
    Mix in flour , sour milk, baking soda , and vanilla .
    flour: 2 cups, baking soda: 1 tsp, vanilla: 1 tsp
  7. 6
    Add in butter and bananas .
    butter: 1/2 cup (melted), bananas: 3 (ripe, mashed)
  8. 7
    Grease and flour a large loaf pan and small loaf pan .
  9. 8
    Fill the pans 2/3 full and bake at 325°F. The small loaf will be done after ⏱️ 45 minutes , and the large loaf will be done after another ⏱️ 15 minutes .
