Hokkaido Milk Bread

.cook
📖 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tc3coiL36Cg

🥘 Ingredients

Tangzhong

  • bread flour
    40 g
  • water
    200 g

Bread

  • active dry yeast
    10 g
  • bread flour (approximately 4 cups)
    580 g
  • egg
    1
  • egg yolk
    1
  • milk (approximately 1 cup and 2 tbsp)
    260 g, 1 splash
  • powdered dry milk (approximately 2.5 tsp)
    10 g
  • salt (approximately 2 tsp)
    12 g
  • sugar (approximately 1/4 cup)
    60 g
  • unsalted butter (approximately 1/2 stick, room temperature)
    50 g

🍳 Cookware

  • stand mixer

Tangzhong

  1. 1
    Mix bread flour with water in a sauce pan. Turn on medium heat and keep stirring until mixture thickens to a paste.
    bread flour: 40 g, water: 200 g
  2. 2
    Refrigerate for ⏱️ 6 hours . Bring out to room temperature ⏱️ 1 hour before use.

Bread

  1. 1
    In a stand mixer with the dough hook attatchment, gently mix bread flour , sugar , salt , active dry yeast , and powdered dry milk .
    bread flour: 580 g (approximately 4 cups), sugar: 60 g (approximately 1/4 cup), salt: 12 g (approximately 2 tsp), active dry yeast: 10 g, powdered dry milk: 10 g (approximately 2.5 tsp)
  2. 2
    At low speed, mix in milk , egg , and tangzhong so that no flour is visible.
    milk: 260 g (approximately 1 cup and 2 tbsp), egg: 1
  3. 3
    Add unsalted butter and continue to mix at low speed.
    unsalted butter: 50 g (approximately 1/2 stick, room temperature)
  4. 4
    Slowly turn up the speed to high and mix until all dough sticks together.
  5. 5
    Take the dough and fold it around and underneath itself, shaping a smooth ball. Place dough in a bowl, cover with plastic wrap, and let it rest for ⏱️ 40-60 minutes .
  6. 📝

    Test the dough by poking your finger down into the middle. If the dough does not spring back, it is ready.

  7. 6
    Divide the dough into 6 equal parts (by weight). Take one piece of dough and fold it over itself. Turn it 90°, slap it on table and fold it over itself again. Using both hands, mold the dough into a ball. Repeat with rest of dough. Cover with plastic wrap and let it rest for ⏱️ 15 minutes .
  8. 7
    Roll each ball of dough out flat. Fold flatten dough using thirds.
  9. 8
    Turn 90°, and roll dough into a roll, pinching it together at the seam. Place 3 molded dough into a baking pan and let it rest/rise for ⏱️ 30 minutes .
  10. 9
    Create an egg wash by whisking egg yolk with milk . Brush the dough with the egg wash and bake in the oven, preheated to 355°F for ⏱️ 25-30 minutes .
    egg yolk: 1, milk: 1 splash
