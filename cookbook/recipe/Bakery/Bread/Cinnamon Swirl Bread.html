Cinnamon Swirl Bread

.cook

🥘 Ingredients

  • active dry yeast (.5oz total, can sub with instant yeast if needed)
    2 pkgs
  • all-purpose flour
    6 cups
  • butter (softened)
    6 tbsp
  • cinnamon
    2 tbsp
  • eggs (room temperature)
    2
  • milk (warm, 110-115°F)
    1 cup
  • salt
    1.5 tsp
  • sugar (can use brown sugar or alternate sugars here)
    1 cup
  • water (warm, 110-115°F)
    1/3 cup

🍳 Cookware

  • stand mixer
  • bowl
  • 9x5 inch loaf pans
  1. 1
    In a stand mixer , dissolve active dry yeast in water .
    active dry yeast: 2 pkgs (.5oz total, can sub with instant yeast if needed), water: 1/3 cup (warm, 110-115°F)
  2. 2
    Add milk , sugar , eggs , butter , salt , and all-purpose flour , then beat on medium until smooth.
    milk: 1 cup (warm, 110-115°F), sugar: 1/2 cup, eggs: 2 (room temperature), butter: 6 tbsp (softened), salt: 1.5 tsp, all-purpose flour: 3 cups
  3. 3
    Gradually add more all-purpose flour and stir to form a soft dough.
    all-purpose flour: 3 cups (Optional)
  4. 4
    Turn dough onto a floured surface; knead until smooth and elastic, about ⏱️ 6-8 minutes .
  5. 📝

    The easiest way to tell if your bread is fully kneaded is to do what bakers call the “windowpane test.” Simply stretch a bit of dough between your fingers, and if the dough tears, keep kneading. If it stretches to form a translucent “window,” the bread is ready to rise, or “proof.”

  6. 5
    Place in a greased bowl, turning once to grease the top. Cover, let rise in a warm place until doubled, about ⏱️ 1 hour .
  7. 6
    Punch down the dough and then turn onto a lightly floured surface.
  8. 7
    Divide the dough in half.
  9. 8
    Roll each portion into an 18x8-inch rectangle.
  10. 9
    Mix sugar with cinnamon in a bowl to create a cinnamon sugar mixture.
    sugar: 1/2 cup (can use brown sugar or alternate sugars here), cinnamon: 2 tbsp
  11. 10
    Sprinkle each rectangle with cinnamon sugar to within 1/2-inch of the edges.
  12. 11
    Roll up jelly-roll style, starting with a short side; pinch seam to seal.
  13. 12
    Place in 2 greased 9x5 inch loaf pans , seam side down.
  14. 13
    Cover with kitchen towels; let rise in a warm place until doubled, about ⏱️ 1.5 hours .
  15. 14
    Preheat oven to 350°F.
  16. 15
    Bake until golden brown, ⏱️ 30-35 minutes .
  17. 16
    Remove from pans to wire racks to cool.
  18. 📝

    Leftover cinnamon bread should be stored at room temperature. Allow it to cool completely, then store in an airtight container, plastic bag or bread box for two to three days. You can also keep cinnamon bread in the refrigerator, where it will last up to five days. Note that the crust will get a bit softer in the fridge than at room temperature.
