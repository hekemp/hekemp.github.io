#Homemade french bread
👥 32 servings 🍽️ Appetizer ⏱️ Prep Time: 1 hour 10 minutes 🔥 Cook Time: 20 minutes 🌍 French 📖 https://www.iheartnaptime.net/homemade-french-bread/

🥘 Ingredients

  • active dry yeast
    1 tablespoon
  • butter (Optional) (melted)
  • egg white (Optional) (whisked)
    1
  • flour
    3 cup, 2 cups
  • olive oil
    1 teaspoon
  • salt
    2.5 teaspoons
  • sugar
    2.5 teaspoons
  • water (warm, about 105°F)
    2 cups, 1 tsp

🍳 Cookware

  • bowl
  • stand mixer
  • baking sheet
  • baking sheet
  1. 1
    In a small bowl , combine water , active dry yeast , and sugar . Let sit ⏱️ 5 minutes , or until it begins to foam.
    water: 2 cups (warm, about 105°F), active dry yeast: 1 tablespoon, sugar: 2.5 teaspoons
  2. 2
    In a stand mixer fitted with a paddle attachment, stir together flour and salt .
    flour: 2 cups, salt: 2.5 teaspoons
  3. 3
    Stir in the yeast mixture on medium-low speed.
  4. 4
    Knead in flour in 1/2 cup increments until the dough is smooth but not sticky. Add more or less flour as needed, use your best judgement.
    flour: 3 cup
  5. 5
    Rub olive oil around the dough ball, cover the bowl with a towel and let rest ⏱️ 15-60 minutes . More time is obviously preferred.
    olive oil: 1 teaspoon
  6. 6
    Turn the dough onto a well-floured surface and divide it in half. Set one half aside.
  7. 7
    Roll the other half into a rectangle (about 15 inches).
  8. 8
    Starting from the long side, roll the dough into a cylinder.
  9. 9
    Turn both ends in and pinch the seams closed.
  10. 10
    Round the edges and place onto a baking sheet .
  11. 11
    Repeat with the second dough ball.
  12. 12
    Make three diagonal cuts across the top of each loaf.
  13. 13
    Cover loaves lightly with a towel. Let rise ⏱️ 30-60 minutes (the longer the better, if you have the time).
  14. 14
    Preheat the oven to 400°F.
  15. 15
    Optionally, combine egg white and water and then brush the top of the dough for a crispier crust.
    egg white: 1 (Optional) (whisked), water: 1 tsp
  16. 16
    Line a baking sheet with a silicone mat or parchment paper. Bake ⏱️ 17-23 minutes , or until the tops are golden brown. When you knock on it, it should sound hollow. If it's browning too fast, lightly cover with foil and lower the temperature to 375°F.
  17. 📝

    You can add a few ice cubes to the oven to help trap the steam and add a few minutes onto the cook time for an egg-less crispier crust.

  18. 17
    Brush the top with butter , if desired for a softer more flavorful crust.
    butter (Optional) (melted)
