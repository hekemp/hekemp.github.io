Slowly Braised Pork Ragu with Pappardelle

.cook
#braised pork #slowly braised recipe #pork shoulder #pasta recipes #comfort food recipes

This Slowly Braised Pork Ragu Pappardelle is the ultimate comfort food meal! Succulent, juicy pork shoulder slowly simmers in a rich tomato sauce until it's fall-apart tender and able to shred with a fork. Finish with a splash of cream and generous sprinkling of parmesan. then toss the pork shoulder ragu into pappardelle pasta for the best homemade Italian meal. Stovetop, oven, slow cooker and electric pressure cooker directions provided.

👥 10 servings 🍽️ Pasta Recipes ⏱️ Prep Time: 25 minutes 🔥 Cook Time: 3 hours 🌍 Italian 👤 Jess Larson 📖 https://playswellwithbutter.com/pork-ragu-recipe

🥘 Ingredients

  • bay leaves
    2
  • carrots (peeled and diced)
    3
  • celery (finely diced)
    1
  • crushed tomatoes
    28 oz can
  • dry white wine
    1 cup
  • garlic (finely chopped)
    8 cloves
  • ground black pepper
    2.5 tsp
  • heavy cream
    1/2 cup
  • kosher salt
    2.5 tsp
  • olive oil
    3 tbsp
  • onion (finely diced)
    1
  • pappardelle
    25 oz
  • parmesan (heaping)
    1/2 cup
  • pork shoulder
    2.5 lbs
  • poultry herb blend (or approx. 2 sprigs fresh rosemary, 4 sprigs fresh sage leaves and 12 sprigs fresh thyme)
    1
  • tomato paste
    2 tbsp
  • water
    1 cup, 2 cups

🍳 Cookware

  • large, heavy-bottomed pot
  • large pot
  1. 1
    Add olive oil to a large, heavy-bottomed pot over medium-high heat. Use paper towel to pat the pork shoulder as dry as possible. Generously season the pork shoulder with kosher salt and ground black pepper . Once the oil in the pot shimmers, carefully add in the seasoned pork shoulder. Work in batches, as necessary, to avoid overcrowding the pot (which prevents browning). Cook for ⏱️ 4-5 minutes per side, until nicely browned. Transfer the browned pork shoulder to a plate and set aside.
    olive oil: 2 tbsp, pork shoulder: 2.5 lbs, kosher salt: 2 tsp, ground black pepper: 2 tsp
  2. 2
    If needed, add the remaining olive oil to the same pot used in Step 1 and reduce heat to medium. Once hot, add in the soffritto ( carrots , onion , and celery ), seasoning with kosher salt and ground black pepper and stirring to combine. Cook, stirring occasionally, until deeply browned, about ⏱️ 15-20 minutes .
    olive oil: 1 tbsp, carrots: 3 (peeled and diced), onion: 1 (finely diced), celery: 1 (finely diced), kosher salt: 1/2 tsp, ground black pepper: 1/2 tsp
  3. 3
    Add the garlic to the pot with the soffritto. Stirring constantly, cook until fragrant, about ⏱️ 1-2 minutes . Add the tomato paste to the pot, stirring to coat the soffritto. Cook for ⏱️ 2-3 minutes , until browned.
    garlic: 8 cloves (finely chopped), tomato paste: 2 tbsp
  4. 4
    Increasing the heat to medium-high, pour the dry white wine into the pot. Stir constantly, using a wooden spoon to scrape up any browned bits that may have formed at the bottom of the pot. Cook for ⏱️ 4-5 minutes , until the wine is almost completely absorbed into the soffritto.
    dry white wine: 1 cup
  5. 5
    Tie the poultry herb blend together using kitchen twine (or finely chop if you do not have twine), then add to the pot with the bay leaves . Add the crushed tomatoes , water (or low-sodium chicken broth or stock), and browned pork shoulder from Step 1. Stir to combine. Bring the mixture to a boil. Reduce heat to maintain a gentle simmer. Cover and cook, stirring occasionally, for ⏱️ 2.5-3 hours , or until the pork shoulder is fall-apart tender. If the ragu begins to reduce too much (i.e. it loses too much of its liquid too quickly), feel free to add in a splash more water and/or reduce the heat further.
    poultry herb blend: 1 (or approx. 2 sprigs fresh rosemary, 4 sprigs fresh sage leaves and 12 sprigs fresh thyme), bay leaves: 2, crushed tomatoes: 28 oz can, water: 2 cups
  6. 6
    Carefully transfer the pork shoulder to a plate or cutting board. At this point, you can remove and discard the spent herbs and bay leaves from the pot, as well. Use tongs or 2 forks to shred the pork shoulder into bite-sized pieces. Return the shredded pork shoulder to the pot with the ragu. Stir to combine.
  7. 7
    Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the pappardelle and cook, stirring occasionally, until the pasta is cooked to al dente according to package directions. Carefully dip a liquid measuring cup into the pot, reserving about water of the starchy pasta water, and set aside. Carefully drain the pappardelle - do NOT rinse it!
    pappardelle: 25 oz, water: 1 cup
  8. 8
    Meanwhile, as the pasta boils, bring the braised pork ragu up to a simmer. Stir in the heavy cream and parmesan . Continue to simmer over low heat, stirring occasionally.
    heavy cream: 1/2 cup, parmesan: 1/2 cup (heaping)
  9. 9
    Add the cooked pappardelle to the pot with the braised pork ragu sauce, tossing to coat. The pork ragu should evenly coat the pasta. Add in some of the reserved pasta water if the ragu needs to loosen up a little; add in an extra handful of parmesan if it needs to tighten up a little. Cook over medium heat for ⏱️ 1-2 minutes , allowing the pasta to meld with and absorb some of the ragu.
  10. 10
    Portion the pork ragu pappardelle into individual pasta bowls, topping with additional grated parmesan, finely chopped fresh herbs, and/or crushed red pepper as desired. Serve immediately. Enjoy!
