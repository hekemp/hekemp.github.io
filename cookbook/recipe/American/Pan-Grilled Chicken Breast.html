Pan-Grilled Chicken Breast

.cook
👥 3 servings 🍽️ Main ⏱️ Prep Time: 10 minutes 🔥 Cook Time: 15 minutes 🌍 American 👤 Nina Cherie Franklin 📖 https://thatsaladlady.com/recipes/salad-toppings/perfect-pan-grilled-chicken-breast-recipe/#wpzoom-premium-recipe-card

🥘 Ingredients

  • black pepper (freshly cracked)
    1/2 tsp
  • chicken breasts (boneless, skinless)
    2
  • dried oregano
    1/2 tsp
  • garlic powder
    1/2 tsp
  • kosher salt
    1 tsp
  • neutral oil
    1 tbsp

🍳 Cookware

  • small bowl
  • skillet
  1. 1
    Place the chicken breasts in a resealable bag. Pound the chicken to an even thickness, about 1/2 inch, starting with the flat side of a meat mallet for consistency and switching to the textured side to tenderize further. A heavy object like a rolling pin works too.
    chicken breasts: 2 (boneless, skinless)
  2. 2
    Pat the tenderized chicken dry with paper towels to remove excess moisture and ensure proper browning.
  3. 3
    In a small bowl , mix kosher salt , black pepper , garlic powder , and dried oregano .
    kosher salt: 1 tsp, black pepper: 1/2 tsp (freshly cracked), garlic powder: 1/2 tsp, dried oregano: 1/2 tsp
  4. 4
    Sprinkle the chicken with the seasonings, pressing them into the surface to help them adhere and enhance the flavor. Repeat on the other side.
  5. 5
    Heat a skillet over medium-high heat and add neutral oil . Spread the oil evenly across the pan and heat until it shimmers but doesn't smoke.
    neutral oil: 1 tbsp
  6. 6
    Add the chicken breasts to the hot pan and cook for ⏱️ 5-6 minutes on the first side without moving them.
  7. 7
    Flip and cook for another ⏱️ 5-6 minutes , or until the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C).
  8. 8
    Transfer to a preheated oven at 400°F (200°C) and bake for ⏱️ 10-15 minutes , or until fully cooked.
  9. 9
    Remove the chicken from the oven and let it rest for ⏱️ 5 minutes .
  10. 📝

    This step allows the juices to redistribute, resulting in tender, flavorful meat.

  11. 10
    Slice the chicken into strips or cubes, depending on your salad needs.
