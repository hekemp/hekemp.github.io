Tater Tot Casserole

.cook
#midwest #midwestern #casserole
👥 8 servings ⏱️ Prep Time: 10 minutes 👤 Francesca Zani 📖 https://www.delish.com/cooking/recipe-ideas/a46305536/easy-tater-tot-casserole-recipe/

🥘 Ingredients

  • Worcestershire sauce
    1 tbsp
  • all-purpose flour
    1 tbsp
  • black pepper
  • cheese (grated, cheddar ideally, grated, cheddar ideally)
    6 oz
  • chicken broth
    3/4 cup
  • cream of mushroom soup (10.5-oz)
    1 can
  • garlic (coarsely chopped)
    4 cloves
  • ground beef
    1.5 lb
  • kosher salt
  • mixed veggies (frozen, at least with corn and green beans)
    12 oz
  • olive oil
    2 tbsp
  • scallions (Optional) (thinly sliced)
  • tater tots (about 72)
    1.5 lb
  • unsalted butter
    2 tbsp
  • yellow mustard (Optional) (optional)
    1 tsp
  • yellow onion (finely chopped)
    1

🍳 Cookware

  • skillet
  • medium bowl
  • 13" x 9" baking dish
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. 2
    Heat a skillet over medium-high heat with olive oil .
    olive oil: 2 tbsp
  3. 3
    Add yellow onion , garlic , kosher salt , and other desired seasonings. Cook, stirring constantly, until lightly browned, about ⏱️ 7-8 minutes .
    yellow onion: 1 (finely chopped), garlic: 4 cloves (coarsely chopped), kosher salt
  4. 4
    Add ground beef , breaking up the meat with a spoon, and cook, stirring frequently, until browned and no longer pink, about ⏱️ 7 minutes . Season with kosher salt , black pepper , and other desired seasonings.
    ground beef: 1.5 lb, kosher salt, black pepper
  5. 5
    Transfer the beef mixture to a medium bowl .
  6. 6
    Reduce heat to medium and melt unsalted butter .
    unsalted butter: 2 tbsp
  7. 7
    Add all-purpose flour and cook, whisking constantly, until the roux is bubbly and fragrant, ⏱️ 1-2 minutes .
    all-purpose flour: 1 tbsp
  8. 8
    Slowly add chicken broth , then whisk constantly until the mixture simmers and thickens; there should be no lumps.
    chicken broth: 3/4 cup
  9. 9
    Stir in cream of mushroom soup . Rinse can out with some additional water or chicken broth to get all the contents out.
    cream of mushroom soup: 1 can (10.5-oz)
  10. 10
    Add the beef mixture, mixed veggies , Worcestershire sauce , yellow mustard , and cheese . Season with kosher salt and other desired seasonings.
    mixed veggies: 12 oz (frozen, at least with corn and green beans), Worcestershire sauce: 1 tbsp, yellow mustard: 1 tsp (Optional) (optional), cheese: 3 oz (grated, cheddar ideally), kosher salt
  11. 11
    Pour the mixture into a 13" x 9" baking dish . Spread evenly with a spoon or spatula.
  12. 12
    Top with cheese .
    cheese: 3 oz (grated, cheddar ideally)
  13. 13
    Arrange tater tots in horizontal rows on top, leaving a small gap between each.
    tater tots: 1.5 lb (about 72)
  14. 14
    Bake the casserole until the center and edges are bubbling, ⏱️ 35-40 minutes . Top with scallions for serving.
    scallions (Optional) (thinly sliced)
