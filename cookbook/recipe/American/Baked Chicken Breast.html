Baked Chicken Breasts

.cook
#chicken #gluten free
👥 4 servings 🍽️ Main Course ⏱️ Prep Time: 20 minutes 🔥 Cook Time: 15 minutes 🌍 American 📖 https://www.gimmesomeoven.com/baked-chicken-breast/

🥘 Ingredients

  • black pepper
    1/2 tsp
  • chicken breasts (boneless skinless, pounded to even thickness)
    4
  • garlic powder
    1/2 tsp
  • kosher salt
    1/4 cup, 1 tsp
  • melted butter or olive oil
  • smoked paprika
    1/2 tsp
  • water (or a few ice cubes)
    4 cups

🍳 Cookware

  • baking dish
  • small bowl
  1. 📝

    Check the label of your chicken breasts before the initial brine, because if your chicken is pre-brined, you should skip this step.

  2. 1
    Fill a large mixing bowl with water of lukewarm water and kosher salt . Stir to combine until most of the salt is absorbed.
    water: 2 cups, kosher salt: 1/4 cup
  3. 2
    Add water to lower the temperature of the water so that it is cool to the touch.
    water: 2 cups (or a few ice cubes)
  4. 3
    Add the chicken breasts and let them sit in the mixture to brine for ⏱️ 15 minutes , or you can also cover the bowl and refrigerate for up to ⏱️ 6 hours .
    chicken breasts: 4 (boneless skinless, pounded to even thickness)
  5. 4
    Remove the chicken breasts from the brine, rinse them with cold water, then pat them dry with paper towels.
  6. 5
    Heat the oven. Preheat oven to 450°F.
  7. 6
    Place the chicken breasts in a single layer in a baking dish .
  8. 7
    In a separate small bowl , whisk kosher salt , black pepper , garlic powder , and smoked paprika until combined.
    kosher salt: 1 tsp, black pepper: 1/2 tsp, garlic powder: 1/2 tsp, smoked paprika: 1/2 tsp
  9. 8
    Brush melted butter or olive oil onto the chicken evenly. Sprinkle the seasoning mixture evenly over the chicken. Flip chicken and repeat on the other side.
    melted butter or olive oil
  10. 9
    Bake for ⏱️ 15-18 minutes , or until the chicken is cooked through and no longer pink.
  11. 📝

    Cooking time will depend on the thickness of the chicken breasts, so I recommend using a cooking thermometer to know exactly when the chicken is fully cooked. The thickest part of the breast should measure 165°F.

  12. 📝

    If you want the chicken to be a little bit browned and crispier on top, you can turn the broiler on high for the final 3-5 minutes of the cooking time and broil the chicken until it is cooked through and golden on top. Keep a close eye on the chicken, however, so that it does not overcook and/or burn.

  13. 10
    Once the chicken is cooked, remove the pan from the oven, transfer the chicken to a clean plate, and loosely tent the plate with aluminum foil. Let the chicken rest for at least ⏱️ 5-10 minutes .
