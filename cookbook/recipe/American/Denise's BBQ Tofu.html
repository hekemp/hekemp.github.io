Denise's BBQ Tofu

.cook
👥 3 servings

🥘 Ingredients

  • barbecue sauce
  • cayenne pepper (Optional)
  • garlic powder (Optional)
  • olive oil
    30 g
  • onion powder (Optional)
  • paprika (Optional)
  • peanut butter (smooth)
    100 g
  • soy sauce (Optional)
  • tofu (extra firm, frozen, then thawed)
    1 lbs

🍳 Cookware

  • bowl
  1. 1
    Pat the tofu dry with a paper towel to absorb the mixture even more.
    tofu: 1 lbs (extra firm, frozen, then thawed)
  2. 2
    Mix peanut butter with olive oil in a bowl .
    peanut butter: 100 g (smooth), olive oil: 30 g
  3. 📝

    You are more so looking for a thick enough marinade to stuff to the tofu without being runny. You may need to adjust this ratio accordingly, but this is our best guess from Denise's vibes.

  4. 3
    Mix in additional spices or flavors of your choice to the marinade, i.e. garlic powder , paprika , onion powder , cayenne pepper , and soy sauce .
    garlic powder (Optional), paprika (Optional), onion powder (Optional), cayenne pepper (Optional), soy sauce (Optional)
  5. 4
    Slice the tofu into 1-inch slabs.
  6. 📝

    For crunchier tofu, do a thinner slice. For softer tofu, do thicker slices.

  7. 5
    Dip the tofu slabs in the marinade, ensuring to coat both sides.
  8. 6
    Let marinate for at least ⏱️ 1-2 hours in the fridge, though you can do overnight if you prefer.
  9. 7
    Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  10. 8
    Bake for ⏱️ 20 minutes .
  11. 9
    Flip the tofu and then bake for another ⏱️ 20 minutes .
  12. 📝

    If the tofu comes out too soft when you flip it, you may want to bump to 375° F, which will give it some extra crunch, but it also raises the risk for burning, so watch it carefully.

  13. 10
    Coat the tofu with barbecue sauce , flipping as needed to cover all sides. Then, put the tofu back in the oven for another ⏱️ 20 minutes .
    barbecue sauce
