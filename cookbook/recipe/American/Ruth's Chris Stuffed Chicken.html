Ruth's Chris Stuffed Chicken

.cook
👥 4 servings 🍽️ Main Dishes ⏱️ Prep Time: 10 minutes 🔥 Cook Time: 30 minutes 👤 Jessica Haggard 📖 https://www.easyhomemadelife.com/ruth-chris-stuffed-chicken/

🥘 Ingredients

  • Worcestershire sauce
    2 teaspoons
  • boneless chicken breast
    4 medium
  • cream cheese
    8 ounces
  • dry Ranch seasoning mix (the garlic one is great)
    1 ounce
  • ground black pepper
    1/4 teaspoon
  • paprika
    1/4 teaspoon
  • salt
    1/2 teaspoon
  • shredded cheddar cheese
    1 cup

🍳 Cookware

  • medium mixing bowl
  • baking sheet
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 450°F (230°C).
  2. 2
    Mix cream cheese , shredded cheddar cheese , dry Ranch seasoning mix , and Worcestershire sauce together in a medium mixing bowl .
    cream cheese: 8 ounces, shredded cheddar cheese: 1 cup, dry Ranch seasoning mix: 1 ounce (the garlic one is great), Worcestershire sauce: 2 teaspoons
  3. 📝

    You can make your own seasoning mix if you prefer. The recommended alternative is: 2 oz buttermilk powder, 2 tbsp dried parsley, 1 tbsp dried chives, 2 tsp garlic powder, 2 tsp onion powder, 1 tsp dried dill, 1/2 tsp salt, and 1/2 tsp ground black pepper. If you use this, it's only about 1/3 you need for 1 batch.

  4. 3
    Divide the mixture into quarters. Roll each portion into a log between the palms of your hands. Aim for a log with tapered ends.
  5. 4
    Open a pocket in each boneless chicken breast by slicing lengthwise with a sharp knife, cut as deep as you can without separating the meat completely. Cut along the rounded edge.
    boneless chicken breast: 4 medium
  6. 5
    Open and stuff one cream cheese log into each of the pockets. Use optional toothpicks to hold the breast meat in place.
  7. 6
    Combine salt , ground black pepper , and paprika in a small bowl. Sprinkle evenly over the tops of the chicken breasts.
    salt: 1/2 teaspoon, ground black pepper: 1/4 teaspoon, paprika: 1/4 teaspoon
  8. 7
    Arrange the stuffed chicken on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper or foil.
  9. 8
    Cook for ⏱️ 30-35 minutes until the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C).
