Smoky Roasted Salsa Roja

.cook

With guidance from Noah TheFiestaDog himself.

👥 6 servings 🌍 Mexican

🥘 Ingredients

  • ancho chiles (dried, stems removed, deseeded)
    2
  • brown sugar (Optional)
    2 tsp
  • garlic (whole)
    4 cloves
  • guajillo chiles (dried, stems removed, deseeded)
    2
  • habaneros (stems removed, deseeded. If you like heat, add up to 6 total)
    2
  • jalapeño peppers (stems removed, deseeded)
    2
  • lime (Optional) (juiced)
    1
  • mango (Optional) (medium, peeled and chopped)
    1
  • olive oil
  • onion (white, just the middle rings)
    2/3
  • roma tomatoes (top and bottom removed only)
    4.5
  • serrano peppers (stems removed, deseeded)
    2
  • water

🍳 Cookware

  • medium pot
  • cast iron pan
  • food processor
  1. 1
    Bring a medium pot of water to a boil.
    water
  2. 2
    Add ancho chiles and guajillo chiles and reduce heat to a simmer. Cook/rehydrate for around ⏱️ 25 minutes or until softened. Be sure to keep the chili water afterwards.
    ancho chiles: 2 (dried, stems removed, deseeded), guajillo chiles: 2 (dried, stems removed, deseeded)
  3. 3
    Place roma tomatoes , onion , jalapeño peppers , serrano peppers , habaneros , rehydrated chiles, and garlic in a cast iron pan . Drizzle with olive oil .
    roma tomatoes: 4.5 (top and bottom removed only), onion: 2/3 (white, just the middle rings), jalapeño peppers: 2 (stems removed, deseeded), serrano peppers: 2 (stems removed, deseeded), habaneros: 2 (stems removed, deseeded. If you like heat, add up to 6 total), garlic: 4 cloves (whole), olive oil
  4. 4
    Broil on the top-most shelf of your oven for ⏱️ 5-10 minutes , or just until the ingredients start to char.
  5. 5
    In a food processor (or blender), combine your ingredients with brown sugar , lime , and mango , about ⏱️ 1 minute . If you prefer chunky salsa, leave some ingredients out until the last few seconds of blending. If you find the salsa to be too thick, add the chili water from earlier.
    brown sugar: 2 tsp (Optional), lime: 1 (Optional) (juiced), mango: 1 (Optional) (medium, peeled and chopped)
  6. 📝

    If you find this still isn't spicy enough for you, replace the guajillo with chile de arbol.

  7. 📝

    I've been told you can follow pretty much any recipe, but don't trust anything the uses oregano. Some sample recipes are: https://www.kitchengidget.com/2016/03/19/guajillo-chile-salsa-recipe/ and https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MUYRIgzSBsI&t=34s
