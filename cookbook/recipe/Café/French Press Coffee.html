French Press Coffee

.cook
👥 2 servings

🥘 Ingredients

  • coffee (coarsely ground)
    35 g
  • hot water (195°F, approximately obtained by boiling water then letting sit for 30 seconds)
    525 g

🍳 Cookware

  • french press
  1. 1
    Prepare your coffee . Pour the grounds into your french press and then pour hot water over the grounds. Set a timer for ⏱️ 4 minutes from when the water first touches the coffee.
    coffee: 35 g (coarsely ground), hot water: 525 g (195°F, approximately obtained by boiling water then letting sit for 30 seconds)
  2. 2
    Press the plunger down until the filter is at the top of the water, just enough to keep the grounds submerged. Stir the french press as desired to keep grounds mixing with coffee.
  3. 3
    Press the plunger down when your timer goes off and serve immediately.
  4. 📝

    The important thing is to preserve a 1g coffee to 15g water ratio.
