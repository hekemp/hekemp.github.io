PB&J Creme Pie Cookies

.cook
#gluten-free #peanut butter
👥 15 servings 👤 Jesse Szewczyk

🥘 Ingredients

  • baking soda
    2 tsp
  • brown sugar
    1.333 cups
  • eggs
    2
  • peanut butter (smooth)
    2 cups
  • salt
    1 tsp
  • sugar
    1 cup
  • vanilla
    1 tbsp

Filling

  • butter (unsalted, softened)
    1 stick
  • confectioners' sugar
    2 cups
  • peanut butter (smooth)
    1/2 cup
  • salt
    1 pinch
  • strawberry jam
    3/4 cup
  • vanilla
    1 tsp

🍳 Cookware

  • baking sheets
  • large bowl
  • stand mixer
  1. 📝

    The source is just a repost of Cookies by Jesse Szewczyk.

  2. 1
    Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  3. 2
    Line 2 baking sheets with parchment paper.
  4. 3
    In a large bowl , combine eggs , vanilla , brown sugar , sugar . Whisk until smooth, pale yellow, and slightly ribbony.
    eggs: 2, vanilla: 1 tbsp, brown sugar: 1.333 cups, sugar: 1/2 cup
  5. 4
    Add peanut butter , baking soda , and salt and stir with a rubber spatula until a thick, smooth dough forms. It should be like Play-Doh.
    peanut butter: 2 cups (smooth), baking soda: 2 tsp, salt: 1 tsp
  6. 5
    Place sugar on a plate.
    sugar: 1/2 cup
  7. 6
    Portion the dough into about 2 tablespoon balls. Roll the balls in the sugar and place at least 3 inches apart on the cookie sheets.
  8. 7
    Press the dough balls down with the palm of your hand, to about 1/2 inch thick.
  9. 8
    Bake both sheets at the same time, swapping midway, until the tops begin to crack, about ⏱️ 9-11 minutes .

Filling

  1. 1
    In a stand mixer with the paddle attachment, combine butter , peanut butter , vanilla , and salt .
    butter: 1 stick (unsalted, softened), peanut butter: 1/2 cup (smooth), vanilla: 1 tsp, salt: 1 pinch
  2. 2
    With the mixer running on low speed, slowly add confectioners' sugar until incorporated ⏱️ 3-4 minutes .
    confectioners' sugar: 2 cups
  3. 3
    Increase the mixer speed to medium-high and whip until fluffy, ⏱️ 1-2 minutes .
  4. 4
    Carefully flip over half the cookies.
  5. 5
    Dollop about 2 tablespoons of the filling onto the the flipped cookies.
  6. 6
    Put some strawberry jam on top of each dollop, around 2 teaspoon per cookie.
    strawberry jam: 3/4 cup
  7. 7
    Put the unflipped cookies on top of the jelly and press very lightly.
  8. 8
    Let sit uncovered at room temperature for ⏱️ 2 hours to let the filling firm up.
  9. 9
    These tend to be a little dense, so I recommend cutting them in half with a knife carefully, cleaning the knife between cuts.
  10. 10
    Keep refrigerated.
