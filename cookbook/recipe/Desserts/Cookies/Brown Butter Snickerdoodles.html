Brown Butter Snickerdoodles

.cook
#snickerdoodles #brown butter #Christmas
👥 18 servings 🍽️ Cookies ⏱️ Prep Time: 20 minutes 🔥 Cook Time: 10 minutes 🌍 American 👤 Jesse Szewczyk 📖 https://alexandracooks.com/2021/12/10/brown-butter-bourbon-snickerdoodles/

🥘 Ingredients

  • baking soda
    1 tsp
  • bourbon (Optional) (whiskey, dark spiced rum, etc. all work)
    2 tbsp
  • brown sugar
    1/2 cup
  • butter (unsalted)
    2 sticks
  • cinnamon
    1 tbsp
  • cream of tartar
    2 tsp
  • eggs (beaten, room temperature)
    2
  • flour
    2.5 cups
  • salt
    1/2 tsp
  • sugar
    1.5 cup
  • vanilla extract
    1 tsp

🍳 Cookware

  • small saucepan
  • sheet pan
  • stand mixer
  1. 📝

    The source is just a repost of Cookies by Jesse Szewczyk.

  2. 1
    Melt butter in a small saucepan over medium heat. Continue cooking, stirring often to prevent burning, until the butter foams and then darkens in color slightly and is very fragrant, about ⏱️ 4 minutes .
    butter: 2 sticks (unsalted)
  3. 2
    Transfer the butter immediately to a heatproof bowl, and let it cool at room temperature for ⏱️ 20 minutes .
  4. 3
    Heat the oven to 350ºF.
  5. 4
    Line two sheet pan with parchment paper.
  6. 5
    Combine flour , cream of tartar , baking soda , salt in a large bowl.
    flour: 2.5 cups, cream of tartar: 2 tsp, baking soda: 1 tsp, salt: 1/2 tsp
  7. 6
    With a paddle attachment on a stand mixer , combine the cooled butter, sugar , and brown sugar . Beat at medium speed until combined, about ⏱️ 1 minute .
    sugar: 1 cup, brown sugar: 1/2 cup
  8. 7
    Turn the mixer off and add eggs , vanilla extract , and bourbon . Mix on medium speed until pale yellow and ribbony, about ⏱️ 2-3 minutes .
    eggs: 2 (beaten, room temperature), vanilla extract: 1 tsp, bourbon: 2 tbsp (Optional) (whiskey, dark spiced rum, etc. all work)
  9. 8
    With the mixer running on low speed, gradually add the flour mixture until just combined.
  10. 9
    In a small bowl, combine sugar and cinnamon .
    sugar: 1/2 cup, cinnamon: 1 tbsp
  11. 10
    Portion out dough into 2 tbsp balls.
  12. 11
    Roll in the cinnamon sugar mixture and place the dough balls at least 3 inches apart on the prepared baking sheets.
  13. 12
    Bake both sheets at the same time for about ⏱️ 8-11 minutes , until the tops of the cookies are lightly browned. Swap the top and bottom sheets midway through baking.
  14. 13
    Let cool slightly on the baking sheets, then transfer to a wire rack to cool.
