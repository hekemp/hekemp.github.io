Chocolate Cheesecake Cookies

.cook
👥 24 servings 🍽️ chocolate, Cookies, Dessert ⏱️ Prep Time: 10 minutes 🔥 Cook Time: 14 minutes 🌍 American 📖 https://familycookierecipes.com/chocolate-cheesecake-cookies

🥘 Ingredients

Dough

  • baking powder
    1 tsp
  • cocoa powder
    1/3 cup
  • cream cheese (softened)
    8 oz
  • eggs
    2
  • flour
    2.25 cups
  • milk
    1/4 cup
  • mini chocolate chips
    2/3 cup
  • regular chocolate chips
    1/4 cup
  • salted butter (softened)
    1/2 cup
  • sea salt
    1 pinch
  • sugar
    1.5 cups
  • vanilla extract
    1 tbsp

Chocolate drizzle

  • regular chocolate chips
    1/4 cup

🍳 Cookware

  • cookie sheets
  • small mixing bowl
  • large bowl

Dough

  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 350*F degrees.
  2. 2
    Line two cookie sheets with parchment paper or silicon baking mats. Set them aside.
  3. 3
    In a small mixing bowl , cream together the salted butter , cream cheese , and milk until smooth.
    salted butter: 1/2 cup (softened), cream cheese: 8 oz (softened), milk: 1/4 cup
  4. 4
    Then, add in the sugar , vanilla extract , and eggs . Mix again to combine well.
    sugar: 1.5 cups, vanilla extract: 1 tbsp, eggs: 2
  5. 5
    In a separate large bowl , combine the flour , cocoa powder , sea salt , and baking powder . Whisk the dry ingredients together.
    flour: 2.25 cups, cocoa powder: 1/3 cup, sea salt: 1 pinch, baking powder: 1 tsp
  6. 6
    Add the wet ingredients to the large bowl of dry ingredients, a bit at a time, mixing well after each addition.
  7. 📝

    Alic didn't have luck with this, he recommended trying dry to wet but maybe the amount really matters, tbd.

  8. 7
    Next, fold in the mini chocolate chips and regular chocolate chips .
    mini chocolate chips: 2/3 cup, regular chocolate chips: 1/4 cup
  9. 8
    Use a medium-sized cookie scoop to scoop the dough out into round balls of dough. Arrange 12 cookies onto each of your prepared cookie sheets.
  10. 9
    Wet your hands slightly and gently press down the tops of the cookies. Tap the cookie dough balls with your fingers to smooth the edges. Top with additional regular chocolate chips as desired.
    regular chocolate chips (Optional)
  11. 10
    Proceed to place the baking sheet in the oven and bake for ⏱️ 12-14 minutes . Allow the cookies to cool on the baking sheet for ⏱️ 5 minutes before transferring them to a wire cooling rack.

Chocolate drizzle

  1. 1
    In a small bowl, measure out the remaining regular chocolate chips and microwave for ⏱️ 1 minute and allow to rest for ⏱️ 2 minutes in the microwave before gently stirring. If necessary, heat for an additional ⏱️ 10-15 seconds to fully melt.
    regular chocolate chips: 1/4 cup
  2. 2
    Using a small spoon, drizzle the chocolate over the top of the baked cookies. Alternatively, you can transfer the chocolate to a small baggie and snip the corner to easily pipe the chocolate over the tops of the cookies.
  3. 3
    Let cool to set for ⏱️ 5-10 minutes , then serve and enjoy.
