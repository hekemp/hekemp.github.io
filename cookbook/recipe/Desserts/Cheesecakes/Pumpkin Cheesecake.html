Pumpkin Cheesecake

.cook
👥 8 servings

🥘 Ingredients

Crust

  • butter (melted)
    1/2 cup
  • graham crackers (crushed, approximately 1.6 wax packages)
    2 cups
  • sugar
    1/2 cup

Filling

  • cream cheese ()
    2 8oz packages
  • eggs
    2
  • pumpkin spice (Optional) (to taste)
  • pure pumpkin (get a standard can size, but know you won't use all of it)
  • sugar
    2/3 cup
  • vanilla
    1 tsp

Topping

  • pumpkin spice (Optional) (to taste)
  • sour cream
    1 cup
  • sugar
    2 tbsp
  • vanilla
    1 tsp

🍳 Cookware

  • food processor
  • 9" pie pan

Crust

  1. 1
    Mix graham crackers , sugar , and butter in a food processor .
    graham crackers: 2 cups (crushed, approximately 1.6 wax packages), sugar: 1/2 cup, butter: 1/2 cup (melted)
  2. 2
    Press the graham cracker mixture into a 9" pie pan until a crust forms. Set aside.

Filling

  1. 1
    Blend cream cheese , eggs , sugar , vanilla , and pumpkin spice together until smooth.
    cream cheese: 2 8oz packages (), eggs: 2, sugar: 2/3 cup, vanilla: 1 tsp, pumpkin spice (Optional) (to taste)
  2. 2
    Add pure pumpkin a few dollops at a time until the mixture is a light/creamy pumpkin color and taste.
    pure pumpkin (get a standard can size, but know you won't use all of it)
  3. 📝

    Making a small batch of pumpkin muffins is a great way to use up the remainder of the can.

  4. 3
    Pour the filling into the pie crust and spread until even. Bake at 375°F for ⏱️ 20 minutes .
  5. 4
    Let stand for ⏱️ 15 minutes .

Topping

  1. 1
    Combine sour cream , sugar , vanilla , and pumpkin spice .
    sour cream: 1 cup, sugar: 2 tbsp, vanilla: 1 tsp, pumpkin spice (Optional) (to taste)
  2. 2
    Evenly spread the topping on, optionally sprinkling some leftover crumb mixture on top if you still have it.
  3. 3
    Return the cheesecake to a 425°F oven for ⏱️ 10 minutes .
  4. 4
    Let cool and chill before serving.
