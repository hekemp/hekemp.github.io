Dulce de Leche Cheesecake

.cook
👥 8 servings

🥘 Ingredients

Crust

  • butter (melted)
    1/2 cup
  • graham crackers (crushed, about 1.5 wax packages)
    1.75 cup
  • sugar
    1/2 cup

Filling

  • brown sugar
    1/3 cup
  • cinnamon
    1/4 tsp
  • cream cheese (softened)
    2 8oz packages
  • dulce de leche
    1 tbsp
  • eggs
    2
  • sour cream
    1/3 cup
  • sugar
    1 tbsp
  • vanilla
    1 tsp

Swirl

  • dulce de leche
    1/2 cup

Topping

  • dulce de leche
    1/3 cup, 1 tbsp
  • sour cream
    1/3 cup
  • vanilla
    1 tsp

🍳 Cookware

  • food processor
  • 10" glass pie pan
  • bowl
  • bowl

Crust

  1. 1
    Combine butter , graham crackers , and sugar in a food processor .
    butter: 1/2 cup (melted), graham crackers: 1.75 cup (crushed, about 1.5 wax packages), sugar: 1/2 cup
  2. 2
    Spread and press into a 10" glass pie pan until a crust forms.

Filling

  1. 1
    Mix cream cheese until smooth.
    cream cheese: 2 8oz packages (softened)
  2. 2
    Mix in eggs , brown sugar , sugar , vanilla , and sour cream .
    eggs: 2, brown sugar: 1/3 cup, sugar: 1 tbsp, vanilla: 1 tsp, sour cream: 1/3 cup
  3. 3
    Split off two spatula scoops of the filling to a separate bowl . Set aside.
  4. 4
    Mix dulce de leche and cinnamon with the filling.
    dulce de leche: 1 tbsp, cinnamon: 1/4 tsp
  5. 5
    Pour half of the filling into the crust, then set the rest aside.

Swirl

  1. 1
    To the two spatula scoops we set aside, add dulce de leche and mix until incorporated.
    dulce de leche: 1/2 cup
  2. 2
    Pour the mixture into the crust and swirl with a skewer until evenly distributed.
  3. 3
    Take the remainder of the filling and pour it on top of the swirl layer, spreading evenly.
  4. 4
    Bake at 375°F for ⏱️ 20 minutes .
  5. 5
    Let stand for ⏱️ 15 minutes .

Topping

  1. 1
    Combine sour cream , dulce de leche , dulce de leche , and vanilla in a bowl .
    sour cream: 1/3 cup, dulce de leche: 1/3 cup, dulce de leche: 1 tbsp, vanilla: 1 tsp
  2. 2
    Carefully spread the topping on top of the cheesecake. If you have leftover crumb mixture, sprinkle that on top now.
  3. 3
    Return the cheesecake to the oven at 425°F for ⏱️ 10 minutes .
  4. 4
    Cool and chill before serving.
  5. 📝

    We weren't 100% satisfied with the ratios/style of the original recipe: https://www.piesandtacos.com/dulce-de-leche-cheesecake/#recipe . Thus, we modified our Pumpkin Cheesecake recipe with some details from that recipe to try and create this.
