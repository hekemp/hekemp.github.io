Japanese Castella Cake

.cook
👥 6 servings 🍽️ Dessert ⏱️ Prep Time: 20 minutes 🔥 Cook Time: 40 minutes 🌍 Japanese 📖 https://www.justonecookbook.com/castella/

🥘 Ingredients

Preparing the Batter

  • bread flour
    100 g
  • eggs (room temperature)
    3 large
  • honey
    3 tbsp
  • mizuame syrup (Optional)
    1 tbsp
  • sparkling sugar
    1/2 tbsp
  • sugar
    100 g
  • water
    2 tbsp

🍳 Cookware

  • 1 lb loaf pan
  • parchment paper
  • small bowl
  • stand mixer
  • sifter or fine-meshed strainer

Preparing the Batter

  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  2. 2
    On your work surface, place your 1 lb loaf pan (I use a 9x5) on top of your sheet of parchment paper . Ensure you tear off enough so that you can fold up all sides to reach just under the top of the sides of your pan.
  3. 3
    Working clockwise, rip slits into the parchment paper so that you can insert it into the loaf pan, folding the excess tag over or removing as necessary. Secure the folded paper onto the edges with stainless steel clips.
  4. 📝

    JustOneCookbook has very in-depth instructions on how to perfectly do this. When I was learning the recipe, I found this just complicated things. At the end of the day, it doesn't have to be perfect, it just has to keep the lining from moving when you mix the batter during baking.

  5. 4
    Combine water and honey in a small bowl . Then, add mizuame syrup . Take your time to dissolve the mizuame. Press down and mash the mizuame with a mini spatula to facilitate dissolving. Do not microwave the mixture as we do not want to increase the temperature of the batter. Set aside.
    water: 2 tbsp, honey: 3 tbsp, mizuame syrup: 1 tbsp (Optional)
  6. 📝

    Be careful with the mizuame syrup, aka glutinous starch syrup. Use the thick type sold in a jar, and skip if you can't find it. This is important, because you shouldn't use the liquidy packet kind found in stores. It's runny and fluid like honey, and it contains too much moisture for this recipe.

  7. 5
    In the bowl of a stand mixer , crack eggs . Add sugar .
    eggs: 3 large (room temperature), sugar: 100 g
  8. 6
    Fit a stand mixer with the whisk attachment and vigorously beat the eggs and sugar on high speed (Speed 8/10) for ⏱️ 3-5 minutes without stopping. If you beat the eggs with a handheld mixer, it will take more time. The beaten eggs will quadruple in volume and have a thick texture and pale yellow color. When you stop the mixer and lift the whisk attachment, the mixture should fall in ribbons.
  9. 7
    Reduce the stand mixer to low speed (around Speed 2). Gradually add the honey mixture to the batter until combined, about ⏱️ 30 seconds .
  10. 8
    Reduce the speed to the lowest speed (usually Stir). Gradually add bread flour with either a sifter or fine-meshed strainer . Hold the strainer's handle with one hand as you gently tap the strainer with the other, and the flour will gradually sift through. Mix until just combined, for about ⏱️ 30 seconds . Do not overmix. When you lift the whisk, the batter should fall in ribbons.
    bread flour: 100 g
  11. 9
    Using a silicone spatula, scrape the batter from the bowl's sides and bottom and gently fold the batter a few times.
  12. 10
    Sprinkle sparkling sugar on the bottom of the lined pan. This sugar will add a crunchy texture to the cake's bottom and help retain moisture. Gently shake the pan to distribute evenly.
    sparkling sugar: 1/2 tbsp
  13. 11
    Pour the batter into the cake pan in just one spot. This helps to minimize air pockets and smooth the top of the batter.
  14. 12
    To level the batter and help remove air pockets, hold the cake pan 2 inches above the counter and drop it flat onto the counter. Then, draw a zigzag line through the batter with a bamboo skewer to further eliminate air bubbles.
  15. 📝

    The first part of cooking castella is a bit of a gauntlet. Ensure you have a water filled squirt bottle and offset spatula on hand before starting. We repeat the steps, unlike JustOneCookbook, to make step tracking clearer.

The Gauntlet: Round 1

  1. 1
    Place the cake pan on the middle rack of the preheated oven at 350ºF (180ºC). Close the oven door and set a timer for ⏱️ 2 minutes .
  2. 2
    After the 2-minute timer beeps, reset the timer to ⏱️ 1 minute and complete the following several steps within the next minute.
  3. 3
    First, open the oven and spray twice above the cake batter with the spray bottle.
  4. 4
    Next, use an offset spatula to quickly mix and circulate the batter 15 times from the left side, without touching the sparkling sugar at the bottom of the pan.
  5. 📝

    This mixing technique, called awakiri (泡切り, “bubble cut“) in Japanese, helps to remove air pockets and even out the batter's temperature so the cake rises evenly without cracking.

  6. 5
    Finally, quickly mix and circulate the batter 15 times from the right side. Shake off the excess batter from the spatula and close the oven door. Reset the timer to ⏱️ 2 minutes .

The Gauntlet: Round 2

  1. 1
    After the 2-minute timer beeps, reset the timer to ⏱️ 1 minute and complete the following several steps within the next minute.
  2. 2
    First, open the oven and spray twice above the cake batter with the spray bottle.
  3. 3
    Next, use an offset spatula to quickly mix and circulate the batter 15 times from the left side, without touching the sparkling sugar at the bottom of the pan.
  4. 4
    Finally, quickly mix and circulate the batter 15 times from the right side. Shake off the excess batter from the spatula and close the oven door. Reset the timer to ⏱️ 2 minutes .

The Gauntlet: Round 3

  1. 1
    After the 2-minute timer beeps, reset the timer to ⏱️ 1 minute and complete the following several steps within the next minute.
  2. 2
    First, open the oven and spray twice above the cake batter with the spray bottle.
  3. 3
    Next, use an offset spatula to quickly mix and circulate the batter 15 times from the left side, without touching the sparkling sugar at the bottom of the pan.
  4. 4
    Finally, quickly mix and circulate the batter 15 times from the right side. Shake off the excess batter from the spatula and close the oven door. Reset the timer to ⏱️ 2 minutes .

The Gauntlet: Round 4

  1. 1
    After the 2-minute timer beeps, reset the timer to ⏱️ 1 minute and complete the following several steps within the next minute.
  2. 2
    First, open the oven and spray twice above the cake batter with the spray bottle.
  3. 3
    Next, use an offset spatula to quickly mix and circulate the batter 15 times from the left side, without touching the sparkling sugar at the bottom of the pan.
  4. 4
    Quickly mix and circulate the batter 15 times from the right side. Shake off the excess batter from the spatula.
  5. 5
    Remove the stainless steel clips. Run the bamboo skewer through the batter. If any of the clips were submerged in the batter, fill in the clip marks in the batter with the skewer.
  6. 6
    Spray water into the oven, then close the oven door and continue to bake for another ⏱️ 30-40 minutes .

Finishing the Cake

  1. 1
    Monitor your cake carefully in the last few minutes of baking. The cake is done if it bounces back when you touch it with your finger.
  2. 📝

    Tip: If your cake top looks wrinkled or cracked, you may have overbaked it. This is why it's important to check earlier to see if it's done. If the top is lighter in color and sticky, you may have underbaked it.

  3. 2
    Remove the castella from the oven. To help prevent shrinkage, drop the pan twice onto the counter to release the steam vapors from the cake.
  4. 3
    Next, invert the cake from the pan onto a nonstick silicone mat. Leave the parchment paper in place. Leave it upside down and let it cool to room temperature, about ⏱️ 45-60 minutes .
  5. 📝

    Tip: If your cake sticks to the silicone mat, you may have underbaked it. Next time, increase the oven temperature or bake it a few minutes longer.

  6. 4
    Once cooled, immediately wrap the cake with its parchment paper in plastic wrap to retain moisture. Then, put the wrapped cake in the refrigerator and store overnight ( ⏱️ 8-12 hours ). This will help the cake develop a fine and moist texture.
  7. 5
    Remove the cake from the refrigerator. Discard the plastic and carefully peel off the parchment paper.
  8. 6
    Trim the sides of the castella to expose the yellow body of the cake. Use a damp towel to wipe off the crumbs from the blade after every cut. Do not trim the top and bottom of the cake.
  9. 7
    Cut the cake crosswise into slices about 3/4 inch thick.
  10. 📝

    To save for later, wrap individual pieces with plastic wrap. Store for up to 3-4 days at room temperature, 5-7 days in the refrigerator, and 1 month in the freezer.
