Angel Food Cake In A Loaf Pan

.cook
👥 6 servings 🍽️ Cake ⏱️ Prep Time: 25 minutes 🔥 Cook Time: 35 minutes 🌍 American 👤 Carla Cardello 📖 https://www.chocolatemoosey.com/angel-food-cake-in-a-loaf-pan/

🥘 Ingredients

  • blueberries (Optional)
  • cornstarch
    1 tablespoon
  • cream of tartar (substitute for lemon juice or white vinegar)
    1 tbsp
  • egg whites (room temperature, clean with no trace of yolk)
    6
  • flour
    1/2 cup
  • salt
    1/4 tsp
  • strawberries (Optional)
  • sugar
    3/4 cup
  • vanilla extract
    1 tsp
  • whipped cream (Optional)

🍳 Cookware

  • stand mixer
  • 9x5 loaf pan
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 325°F.
  2. 2
    Sift together sugar , flour , and cornstarch .
    sugar: 1/4 cup, flour: 1/2 cup, cornstarch: 1 tablespoon
  3. 3
    In the clean bowl of a stand mixer with the whisk attachment (or with a hand mixer), beat on medium speed the egg whites , cream of tartar , vanilla extract , and salt until foamy, about ⏱️ 30 seconds .
    egg whites: 6 (room temperature, clean with no trace of yolk), cream of tartar: 1 tbsp (substitute for lemon juice or white vinegar), vanilla extract: 1 tsp, salt: 1/4 tsp
  4. 4
    Increase the speed to high. Gradually beat in sugar , 1 tablespoon at a time, while the mixer runs. Beat until soft peaks form (when you pull the beater up, peaks will form but fall over), about ⏱️ 3-4 minutes . Do not beat into stiff peaks.
    sugar: 1/2 cup
  5. 5
    Using a rubber spatula, gently fold in the flour ingredient, 1/4 at a time, into the egg whites. You want to incorporate all of the flour without deflating the egg whites, so take your time. Make sure there is no dry flour left.
  6. 6
    Transfer the batter to an ungreased 9x5 loaf pan and evenly spread. Bake ⏱️ 35-40 minutes or until brown and no longer sticky on top.
  7. 📝

    It is important that you do not line the loaf pan with parchment paper or grease the pan. You need the cake to stick to the pan.

  8. 7
    Turn the pan upside down and rest the handles on top of two jars, canned goods, similar setup. Let cool upside down for ⏱️ 1 hour . Do not skip this step. The cake will deflate if cooled right-side up.
  9. 8
    Once ready to serve, carefully run a butter knife around the edges. Lay the pan on its side then carefully pull the cake out with your knife. If you're having trouble, you can use an offset spatula and run it under the bottom of the cake so it doesn't stick anymore.
  10. 9
    Slice the angel food cake by running a serrated knife under hot water between cutting each slice. Use a gentle sawing motion rather than pushing it down.
  11. 10
    Serve with strawberries , blueberries , and whipped cream .
    strawberries (Optional), blueberries (Optional), whipped cream (Optional)
  12. 📝

    Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days, in the refrigerator for up to 1 week, or in the freezer for up to 4-6 months.
