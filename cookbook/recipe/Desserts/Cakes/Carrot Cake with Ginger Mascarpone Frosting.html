Carrot Cake with Ginger Mascarpone Frosting

.cook
👥 8 servings 🍽️ Dessert 📖 https://barefootcontessa.com/recipes/carrot-cake-with-ginger-mascarpone-frosting

🥘 Ingredients

Ginger Mascarpone Frosting

  • confectioners' sugar (sifted)
    2 cups
  • cream cheese (room temperature)
    4 ounces
  • crystallized ginger (not in syrup)
    1/8 cup
  • heavy cream
    2 tablespoons
  • kosher salt
    1/4 teaspoon
  • mascarpone cheese (room temperature)
    12 ounces
  • vanilla extract
    1/2 teaspoon

Carrot Cake

  • Ginger Mascarpone Frosting
  • all-purpose flour
    2 cups, 1 tablespoon
  • baking soda
    2 teaspoons
  • carrots (grated)
    1 pound
  • chopped walnuts
    1 cup
  • crystallized ginger (chopped)
  • eggs (room temperature)
    3 extra large
  • ground cinnamon
    2 teaspoons
  • kosher salt
    1.5 teaspoons
  • raisins
    1 cup
  • sugar
    2 cups
  • vanilla extract
    1 teaspoon
  • vegetable oil
    1.333 cups

🍳 Cookware

  • two 9 x 2-inch round cake pans
  • electric mixer

Ginger Mascarpone Frosting

  1. 1
    In the bowl of an electric mixer fitted with the paddle attachment, beat mascarpone cheese , cream cheese , confectioners' sugar , heavy cream , and vanilla extract together for about ⏱️ 1 minute , until light and fluffy.
    mascarpone cheese: 12 ounces (room temperature), cream cheese: 4 ounces (room temperature), confectioners' sugar: 2 cups (sifted), heavy cream: 2 tablespoons, vanilla extract: 1/2 teaspoon
  2. 2
    Add crystallized ginger and kosher salt and beat for ⏱️ 30 seconds more.
    crystallized ginger: 1/8 cup (not in syrup), kosher salt: 1/4 teaspoon

Carrot Cake

  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 400 degrees.
  2. 2
    Line the bottoms of two 9 x 2-inch round cake pans with parchment paper, and grease and flour the pans.
  3. 3
    In the bowl of an electric mixer fitted with the paddle attachment, beat sugar , vegetable oil , and eggs on medium-high speed for ⏱️ 2 minutes , until light yellow and thickened. Stir in vanilla extract .
    sugar: 2 cups, vegetable oil: 1.333 cups, eggs: 3 extra large (room temperature), vanilla extract: 1 teaspoon
  4. 4
    In another bowl, sift together all-purpose flour , ground cinnamon , baking soda , and kosher salt . With the mixer on low, slowly add the dry ingredients to the wet ones.
    all-purpose flour: 2 cups, ground cinnamon: 2 teaspoons, baking soda: 2 teaspoons, kosher salt: 1.5 teaspoons
  5. 5
    In a medium bowl, toss carrots , raisins , chopped walnuts , and all-purpose flour . Stir into the batter with a rubber spatula.
    carrots: 1 pound (grated), raisins: 1 cup, chopped walnuts: 1 cup, all-purpose flour: 1 tablespoon
  6. 6
    Divide the batter between the prepared pans and smooth the tops. Bake for ⏱️ 10 minutes , lower the heat to 350 degrees, and bake for ⏱️ 30-35 minutes , until a toothpick comes out clean. Cool in the pans for ⏱️ 15 minutes , turn out onto a baking rack, and cool completely.
  7. 7
    Place one cake on a flat serving plate, rounded side down. Spread half the Ginger Mascarpone Frosting on the top (not the sides). Place the second cake on top of the first cake, rounded side up. Frost just the top of the second cake. Sprinkle with crystallized ginger and serve at room temperature.
    Ginger Mascarpone Frosting, crystallized ginger (chopped)
