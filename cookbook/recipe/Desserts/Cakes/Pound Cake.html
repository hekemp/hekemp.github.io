Pound Cake

.cook
👥 6 servings 📖 https://www.abigmouthful.com/recipe/dorie-greenspan-pound-cake/

🥘 Ingredients

  • baking powder
    1 tsp
  • butter (unsalted, room temperature)
    2 sticks
  • eggs (room temperature)
    4
  • flour (can sub 2.5 cup cake flour)
    2 cups
  • salt
    1/4 tsp
  • sugar
    1 cup
  • vanilla extract
    1 tsp

🍳 Cookware

  • 9x5 inch or 8.5x4.5 inch loaf pan
  • stand mixer
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 325° F.
  2. 2
    Butter a 9x5 inch or 8.5x4.5 inch loaf pan . Put the pan on an insulated baking sheet or two regular baking sheets stacked on top of each other.
  3. 3
    Whisk together flour , baking powder , and salt .
    flour: 2 cups (can sub 2.5 cup cake flour), baking powder: 1 tsp, salt: 1/4 tsp
  4. 4
    Using a stand mixer with a paddle attachment, beat butter and sugar on high speed until fluffy, about ⏱️ 5 minutes .
    butter: 2 sticks (unsalted, room temperature), sugar: 1 cup
  5. 📝

    For some fun variants, try adding vanilla or almond extract (~1/2 tsp of either) or rub the grated zest of 1 lemon or 1/2 orange into the sugar before beating the sugar with the butter.

  6. 5
    Scrape down the bowl and beater and reduce the speed to medium.
  7. 6
    Add eggs one at a time, beating for ⏱️ 1-2 minutes for each egg. Remember to scrape the sides frequently.
    eggs: 4 (room temperature)
  8. 7
    Mix in vanilla extract .
    vanilla extract: 1 tsp
  9. 8
    Reduce mixer speed to low and add the flour mixture, mixing only until it's incorporated. You may even want to just hand mix at this stage.
  10. 9
    Scrape the batter into the buttered pan and smooth the top.
  11. 10
    If you're using a 9x5 pan, you'll need to bake the cake for ⏱️ 70-75 minutes . The smaller pan needs about ⏱️ 90 minutes .
  12. 📝

    Check in on the cake after 45 minutes, and if it's browning too quickly, cover it loosely with a foil tent. You'll know the cake is done when a toothpick comes out clean.

  13. 11
    Remove the cake from the oven and transfer the pan to a rack to rest for ⏱️ 30 minutes .
  14. 12
    Using a blunt knife, remove the cake from the pan and return to the rack to cool to room temperature. Once cool, wrap it tightly in plastic wrap, and leave it on the counter overnight.
  15. 📝

    Wrapped well, the cake will keep for 5-7 days at room temperature or up to 2 months in the freezer.

Marbled Pound Cake Variant

  1. 1
    After the batter is mixed, transfer half of it to another bowl and gently blend in 4oz melted (then cooled) bittersweet chocolate. Alternate large spoonfuls of the light and dark batters into the pan, running a kitchen knife in a zigzag pattern through the batters to marble them.

Cocoa Pound Cake Variant

  1. 1
    Reduce the amount of flour to 1 2/3 cup all-purpose (or 1 3/4 cups cake flour) and sift 1/2 cup unsweetened cocoa with the flour, baking powder, and salt.
