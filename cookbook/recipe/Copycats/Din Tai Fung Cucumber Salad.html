Din Tai Fung Cucumber Salad

.cook
#asian cucumber salad #cucumber salad #din tai fung
👥 3 servings 🍽️ Salad ⏱️ Prep Time: 4 hours 🌍 Asian 📖 https://platingsandpairings.com/din-tai-fung-cucumber-salad-recipe

🥘 Ingredients

  • chili oil (or more, to taste)
    1/2 tbsp
  • fresno chile pepper (minced)
    1
  • garlic (grated or minced, about 1tbsp for 3 cloves)
    3 cloves
  • kosher salt
    1½ tbsp, ½ teaspoon
  • mirin
    1½ tbsp
  • persian cucumbers (sliced into 1/2-inch thick pieces. Can substitute with 2 english cucumbers per 8 persian)
    8
  • rice vinegar
    3 tbsp
  • sesame oil
    1½ tbsp
  • sugar
    2 tbsp
  1. 1
    Add persian cucumbers to a small bowl.
    persian cucumbers: 8 (sliced into 1/2-inch thick pieces. Can substitute with 2 english cucumbers per 8 persian)
  2. 2
    Sprinkle with kosher salt and toss to combine. Let sit for ⏱️ 30 minutes in the refrigerator.
    kosher salt: 1½ tbsp
  3. 3
    After ⏱️ 30 minutes , rinse off the salt mixture and pat the cucumbers dry.
  4. 4
    Meanwhile, make the dressing. In a small bowl, whisk together rice vinegar , mirin , sugar , sesame oil , garlic , and kosher salt . Mix well and set aside.
    rice vinegar: 3 tbsp, mirin: 1½ tbsp, sugar: 2 tbsp, sesame oil: 1½ tbsp, garlic: 3 cloves (grated or minced, about 1tbsp for 3 cloves), kosher salt: ½ teaspoon
  5. 5
    Toss the rinsed and dried cucumbers with the dressing and let marinate for a minimum of ⏱️ 4 hours , up to ⏱️ 2 days .
  6. 6
    To serve: Taste for seasoning, and add additional kosher salt if needed. Drizzle with chili oil and sprinkle with fresno chile pepper (optional) and serve chilled.
    kosher salt, chili oil: 1/2 tbsp (or more, to taste), fresno chile pepper: 1 (minced)
