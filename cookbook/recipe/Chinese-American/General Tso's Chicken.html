General Tso's Chicken

.cook
👥 3 servings 🌍 American Chinese 📖 https://www.recipetineats.com/general-tsos-chicken/

🥘 Ingredients

  • rice (Optional) (jasmine preferred)

Sauce-Marinade

  • brown sugar
    3 tbsp
  • chicken stock/broth
    3/4 cup
  • chili paste (Sambal Oelak preferred)
    3 tsp
  • cornflour/cornstarch
    1 tbsp
  • hoisin sauce
    1 tbsp
  • rice vinegar (or white vine)
    1 tbsp
  • soy sauce (light or all-purpose)
    3 tbsp
  • toasted sesame oil
    1 tsp

Chicken Prep

  • chicken thighs (boneless skinless, cut into 2.5cm / 1" pieces)
    1.4 lbs
  • garlic (finely grated)
    1 tsp
  • ginger (finely grated)
    1 tsp

Frying the Chicken

  • cornflour/cornstarch
    3/4 cup
  • garlic (finely chopped)
    2 cloves
  • ginger (finely chopped)
    2 tsp
  • green onion (Optional) (finely sliced)
  • oil
    2 tbsp
  • red chilli flakes
    2 tsp
  • sesame seeds (Optional)

🍳 Cookware

  • bowl
  • small bowl
  • deep fryer
  • oven
  • large skillet
  1. 1
    If you want to serve with rice , start your rice prep now.
    rice (Optional) (jasmine preferred)

Sauce-Marinade

  1. 1
    Mix soy sauce , hoisin sauce , rice vinegar , chili paste , and toasted sesame oil in a bowl .
    soy sauce: 3 tbsp (light or all-purpose), hoisin sauce: 1 tbsp, rice vinegar: 1 tbsp (or white vine), chili paste: 3 tsp (Sambal Oelak preferred), toasted sesame oil: 1 tsp
  2. 2
    Take out 2tbsp from the sauce mixture and put in a small bowl . Set aside.
  3. 3
    To the remaining sauce-marinade, mix in brown sugar and cornflour/cornstarch .
    brown sugar: 3 tbsp, cornflour/cornstarch: 1 tbsp
  4. 4
    Mix in chicken stock/broth .
    chicken stock/broth: 3/4 cup

Chicken Prep

  1. 1
    In the set aside bowl with 2 tbsp of sauce, add chicken thighs .
    chicken thighs: 1.4 lbs (boneless skinless, cut into 2.5cm / 1" pieces)
  2. 2
    Add ginger and garlic to chicken, mix, then marinate ⏱️ 30 minutes .
    ginger: 1 tsp (finely grated), garlic: 1 tsp (finely grated)

Frying the Chicken

  1. 1
    Put cornflour/cornstarch on a plate. Piece by piece, roll the chicken in the cornflour. Then, using your hands as a cup, shake so the excess cornflour falls through your fingers.
    cornflour/cornstarch: 3/4 cup
  2. 2
    Heat oil in deep fryer to 350°F.
    oil
  3. 3
    In small batches, deep fry the chicken for ⏱️ 3-4 minutes , until golden brown and crispy.
  4. 📝

    When chicken is done, it should begin to float. You may need to shake the pieces a bit if they get stuck to see if they are floating.

  5. 4
    Drain chicken on a paper towel lined plate.
  6. 5
    Preheat oven to 200°F and move the chicken there to keep warm.
  7. 6
    Heat oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add garlic , ginger , and red chilli flakes . Sauté for ⏱️ 30 seconds until garlic is light golden.
    oil: 2 tbsp, garlic: 2 cloves (finely chopped), ginger: 2 tsp (finely chopped), red chilli flakes: 2 tsp
  8. 7
    Add sauce, bring to simmer and stir occasionally until it thickens enough that you can draw a path across pan base, about ⏱️ 7-9 minutes .
  9. 8
    Add chicken, tossing to coat quickly.
  10. 📝

    The quicker you are, the crispier the chicken stays.

  11. 9
    Transfer to serving plate, garnish with green onion and sesame seeds , and serve immediately.
    green onion (Optional) (finely sliced), sesame seeds (Optional)
