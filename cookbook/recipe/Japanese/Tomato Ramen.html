Tomato Ramen

.cook
📖 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pyARBAXm8BA

🥘 Ingredients

Tare

  • konbu
    30 g
  • mirin
    50 mL
  • sake
    50 mL
  • salt
    15 g
  • shiitake mushrooms (dried)
    10 g
  • sugar
    7 g
  • usukuchi shoyu
    300 mL
  • water
    650 mL

Broth

  • chicken bones
    4.5 lbs
  • onion
    1
  • pork belly (tied up for chashu)
    1
  • red wine
    100 mL
  • tomatoes (canned for convenience)
    800 g
  • water (cold)
    1.5 kg

Chashu

  • garlic (Optional)
  • green onion (Optional) (chopped)
  • sake
    50 mL
  • shoyu
    250 mL
  • sugar
    40 g

Serving

  • olive oil (Optional)
  • pepper (Optional)

🍳 Cookware

  • small pot
  • large stock pot
  • mesh strainer
  • small pot
  1. 📝

    For simplicity in the recipe, we skipped the custom noodles, but refer to the source if you prefer.

Tare

  1. 1
    Soak konbu and shiitake mushrooms in water overnight.
    konbu: 30 g, shiitake mushrooms: 10 g (dried), water: 650 mL
  2. 2
    Combine sake and mirin in a small pot . Bring to a boil over medium-high heat.
    sake: 50 mL, mirin: 50 mL
  3. 3
    Add the konbu/mushroom water, usukuchi shoyu , salt , and sugar and mix.
    usukuchi shoyu: 300 mL, salt: 15 g, sugar: 7 g
  4. 4
    Cover the pot and leave on low for ⏱️ 40 minutes .
  5. 📝

    The original recipe calls for cooking at 80°C for 40 minutes, but since we do not have a fancy set-up like that, we go the best intention route.

  6. 5
    Drain the tare into a bowl and store for later use.
  7. 📝

    You do not (and should not) strain the liquid out of the mushrooms or konbu.

Broth

  1. 1
    Clean chicken bones .
    chicken bones: 4.5 lbs
  2. 📝

    Generally, if you're unfamiliar with this process, just rinse it in water and tug off any concerning bits you see.

  3. 2
    Place the bones in a large stock pot and cover with water . Bring to a boil over medium-high heat.
    water: 1.5 kg (cold)
  4. 📝

    Do not try to save time with warm water, it is important that you bring the water to a boil from cold.

  5. 3
    After around ⏱️ 15 minutes , you should start seeing lye on the top of the pot. Skim this with a ladle. Repeat over time as more lye appears.
  6. 📝

    In total, this is around maybe 30 minutes of cooking before the lye stops appearing.

  7. 4
    Add onion {chopped} and pork belly to the pot.
    onion: 1, pork belly: 1 (tied up for chashu)
  8. 📝

    If you're unfamiliar with this process, you roll the pork belly into a tight log and secure it with butcher's twine at regular intervals so it holds its shape during braising. If you don't have the pork belly or generally are skipping the chashu, it is crucial you at least replace it with some amount of pork fat to still give you the fatty goodness.

  9. 5
    Cover and simmer on low for ⏱️ 90 minutes .
  10. 6
    Add tomatoes .
    tomatoes: 800 g (canned for convenience)
  11. 📝

    Remember to splash a little water into the can for all the tomato-y goodness. We found the resulting ramen wasn't super tomato-y, so if you like tomato, you may want to go harder with the tomatoes here.

  12. 7
    Bring the pot back to a rolling boil over medium-high.
  13. 8
    Skim the fat from the pot again until it settled down.
  14. 9
    Mix in red wine .
    red wine: 100 mL
  15. 10
    Cover and reduce heat to low, leaving to simmer for ⏱️ 90 minutes .
  16. 11
    Carefully remove the pork belly and set aside.
  17. 12
    Using a mesh strainer , press all of the juices out into a large bowl. Set aside for later use.

Chashu

  1. 1
    In a small pot , mix shoyu , sugar , and sake . Heat over medium-low until steaming.
    shoyu: 250 mL, sugar: 40 g, sake: 50 mL
  2. 2
    Add green onion and garlic to taste. Let cook for ⏱️ 1-2 minutes , then mix.
    green onion (Optional) (chopped), garlic (Optional)
  3. 3
    Pour the mixture into a gallon freezer bag with the cooked pork belly. Roll up the bag to remove excess air and ensure the pork will completely marinate.
  4. 4
    Let the pork marinate over night in the fridge.

Serving

  1. 1
    Add 1-2 tbsp of the tare to a bowl with 1.25-1.75 cup broth, tuned to your preference on strength. Sprinkle with pepper and olive oil and garnish with other toppings as desired.
    pepper (Optional), olive oil (Optional)
