Japanese Chicken Curry

.cook
#curry roux #japanese curry

Delicious Japanese chicken curry recipe for a weeknight dinner! Tender pieces of chicken, carrots and potatoes cooked in a rich savory curry sauce, this Japanese version of curry is a must-have for your family meal.

👥 8 servings 🍽️ Main Dish ⏱️ Prep Time: 20 minutes 🔥 Cook Time: 50 minutes 🌍 Japanese 📖 https://www.justonecookbook.com/simple-chicken-curry/

  • garlic powder (Optional)

    4 cups
  • curry roux
    1 package
  • fukujinzuke (Optional) (Japanese red pickled vegetables)
  • honey
    1 Tbsp
  • ketchup
    1 Tbsp
  • oil
    1.5 Tbsp
  • soy sauce
    1 Tbsp

  1. 📝

    For a Beef Curry variant, swap chicken with ground beef and chicken stock/broth with beef stock/broth for a beef curry. We recommend swapping to Kokumaro (the whole box) and adding a few cubes of Java Curry to taste (usually half).

Preparation

  1. 1
    In a large bowl , soak potato in water for ⏱️ 15 minutes to remove excess starch.
    potato: 3 (Yukon gold, peeled and quartered), water
  2. 📝

    You can replace with 2 russet potatoes, but they tend to dissolve, so you have to add them later than yukon gold, at the last 15-20 minutes of cooking. We prefer to stick with yukon gold.

  3. 2
    Prepare onion , chunking if you like chunks of onions, mincing/dicing if you prefer just the taste, as then they'll dissolve into the sauce. Place in a bowl and set aside.
    onion: 2
  4. 3
    Grate ginger and set aside in a bowl .
    ginger: 1 tsp (skinned)
  5. 4
    Using a garlic press , press garlic into the same bowl as the ginger.
    garlic: 2 cloves
  6. 5
    Start your rice .
    rice: 2 cups (calrose)
  7. 6
    Grate apple into a bowl .
    apple: 1 (small, peeled, quartered, preferably sugarbee or Fuji)
  8. 7
    Cut carrot into rolling wedges, a style called rangiri in Japanese. Put these in the same bowl as the potatoes, once you've drained them.
    carrot: 2 (peeled)
  9. 8
    Cut off the extra fat from chicken . Season with salt and black pepper with other desired seasonings, i.e. chili powder or garlic powder and let rest in a small bowl .
    chicken: 1.5 lb (breast or thigh, cubed or diagonally sliced as in sogigiri style), salt, black pepper, chili powder (Optional), garlic powder (Optional)

Cooking

  1. 1
    Heat oil in a stockpot over medium heat and add the onion.
    oil: 1.5 Tbsp
  2. 2
    Sauté the onions, stirring occasionally, until they become translucent and tender, about ⏱️ 5 minutes . If you have the time, another ⏱️ 5 minutes to cook the onions more adds more flavor.
  3. 📝

    If you have extra time, definitely caramelize the onions on low heat to add more color and flavor.

  4. 3
    Add the minced garlic and grated ginger and mix well together.
  5. 4
    Add the chicken and cook, stirring frequently, until it's no longer pink on the outside. If the onions are getting too brown, reduce the heat to medium low temporarily.
  6. 5
    Add chicken stock/broth
    chicken stock/broth: 4 cups (to lower sodium, substitute for water)
  7. 6
    Add the grated apple, honey , soy sauce , and ketchup .
    honey: 1 Tbsp, soy sauce: 1 Tbsp, ketchup: 1 Tbsp
  8. 7
    Add the carrots and Yukon gold potatoes The broth should barely cover the ingredients.
  9. 8
    Simmer, covered, on medium-low heat for ⏱️ 15 minutes , stirring occasionally. Simmer uncovered if the ingredients are completely submerged in the broth.
  10. 9
    Once boiling, use a fine-mesh strainer to skim the scum and foam from the surface of the broth.
  11. 10
    Continue to cook covered until a wooden skewer goes through the carrots and potatoes.
  12. 11
    Turn off the heat. From curry roux , put 1-2 cubes in a ladleful of cooking liquid. Slowly let it dissolve with a spoon or chopsticks and stir into the pot to incorporate. Repeat with the rest of the blocks, 2 cubes at a time.
    curry roux: 1 package
  13. 📝

    We find mixing roux packages is the best way to go. House Foods is a classic, we usually do half Vermont Curry and half Java Curry. Add 1-2 extra cubes if you like a thicker roux.

  14. 12
    Simmer, uncovered, on medium-low heat, stirring frequently, until the curry becomes thick, about ⏱️ 5-10 minutes . If your curry is too thick, you can add water to thin the sauce. When you stir, make sure that no roux or food is stuck to the bottom of the pot; otherwise, it may burn.
  15. 13
    Serve with your rice and fukujinzuke .
    fukujinzuke (Optional) (Japanese red pickled vegetables)

Modifications and Making It Your Own

  1. 📝

    What makes your curry stand out is usually what 'secret ingredients' you add. JustOneCookbook recommends apple, honey, soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and ketchup, which we keep here, but they also provide the following options. We recommend starting with just 1-3 tsp of any of these options.

  2. 1
    For a sweet taste, use Chutney, Grated apple, Honey (Amylase in honey breaks starch and causes viscosity loss; therefore, add honey with broth and cook it. Do not add it after the curry roux as the curry will become watery.), Ketchup, Mashed banana, Mirin, or Orange/Apricot jam.
  3. 2
    For a refreshing/sour taste, use Lemon (Add right before serving), Tomato, or Yogurt.
  4. 3
    For a spicy taste, use Red chili pepper, Black pepper, or Cayenne pepper.
  5. 4
    For fragrance, use cumin, Bay leaf, Cinnamon, or Curry leaves.
  6. 5
    For umami and depth, use Butter (Add with roux), Chocolate (Add with roux), Garlic cloves (Add while sauteeing), Fresh ginger (Add while sauteeing), Instant coffee, Milk (Add with roux), Oyster sauce, Red wine, Sake, Soy sauce, Tonkatsu sauce, or Worcestershire Sauce.
