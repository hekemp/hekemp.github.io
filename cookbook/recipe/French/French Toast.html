French Toast

.cook
#basic french toast recipe #Best bread for French toast #best french toast #best french toast recipe #classic french toast #Easy French toast #easy french toast recipe #french toast #French toast bread #French toast ideas #French toast ingredients #French toast recipe easy #How to make French toast

This easy French Toast recipe is the BEST, with a thicker batter and cinnamon sugar flavors that really set it apart. It's one of our family's favorite breakfasts!

👥 8 servings 🍽️ Breakfast ⏱️ Prep Time: 5 minutes 🔥 Cook Time: 10 minutes 🌍 French 📖 https://tastesbetterfromscratch.com/classic-french-toast/

🥘 Ingredients

  • bread
    8 slices
  • cinnamon (ground)
    1 tsp
  • eggs (large)
    4
  • flour (all-purpose)
    1/4 cup
  • milk
    2/3 cup
  • powdered sugar (Optional)
  • salt
    1/4 tsp
  • sugar (granulated)
    1/4 cup
  • syrup
  • vanilla extract
    1 tsp

🍳 Cookware

  • skillet
  • shallow dish
  1. 1
    Preheat the skillet over medium heat.
  2. 2
    In a shallow dish , mix flour and eggs together.
    flour: 1/4 cup (all-purpose), eggs: 4 (large)
  3. 3
    Whisk in milk , sugar , salt , cinnamon , and vanilla extract until smooth.
    milk: 2/3 cup, sugar: 1/4 cup (granulated), salt: 1/4 tsp, cinnamon: 1 tsp (ground), vanilla extract: 1 tsp
  4. 4
    Dip bread into the batter, dredging them well on both sides, and place on hot skillet.
    bread: 8 slices
  5. 5
    Cook for a few minutes, until the bottom of the breads starts to get golden brown. Flip and cook on the other side the same.
  6. 6
    Remove to a plate. Serve warm with syrup and powdered sugar .
    syrup, powdered sugar (Optional)
