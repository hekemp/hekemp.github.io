Stir Fry

.cook

🥘 Ingredients

Basic Outline

  • aromatics (Optional)
    1 tbsp
  • lemon juice (Optional)
  • lime juice (Optional)
  • oil
    3.5 tbsp
  • pepper
  • protein
    1 lbs
  • salt
  • stir-fry sauce
  • vegetables
    4 cups

🍳 Cookware

  • wok
  1. 📝

    This is a very generic plug-and-play type recipe. Our go-to is the Orange-Ginger Sauce with chicken breast, garlic, green onions, onions, bell peppers, baby corn, snow peas, and sugar snap peas. Customize as you see fit.

Basic Outline

  1. 1
    If using chicken, pork, or steak as protein, season with salt , pepper , and other seasonings to taste. Tenderize with a fork.
    salt, pepper
  2. 2
    Heat a wok over medium-high heat. Add oil .
    oil: 2 tbsp
  3. 3
    Add protein and let sit for ⏱️ 2-4 minutes before tossing to coat in oil.
    protein: 1 lbs
  4. 4
    Saute until proteins are golden. Remove from pan and set aside. The protein does not have to be cooked all the way yet.
  5. 5
    Wipe wok with a paper towel and return to medium-high heat. Add oil to the wok.
    oil: 1.5 tbsp
  6. 6
    Add aromatics . Once you can smell them, add vegetables to heated oil with a sprinkle of salt and stir-fry for ⏱️ 10 minutes , until tender but still crunchy.
    aromatics: 1 tbsp (Optional), vegetables: 4 cups, salt
  7. 📝

    If the pan ever looks too dry and ingredients may burn, add a splash of water or stock

  8. 7
    Add protein back to the wok and push all ingredients to the sides, forming a hole in the middle.
  9. 8
    Stir chosen stir-fry sauce and then pour into the hole.
    stir-fry sauce
  10. 9
    Wait for sauce to bubble and darken before tossing everything through and making sure all ingredients are coated.
  11. 10
    Cook until all ingredients are tender and then remove from heat.
  12. 11
    Add lemon juice , lime juice , and additional seasonings to taste.
    lemon juice (Optional), lime juice (Optional)
  13. 12
    Top with desired optional garnishes while serving.

Basic Sauce

  1. 1
    Combine: 3 cloves garlic, 3 tablespoons soy sauce, 1 tablespoon rice vinegar, 1 tablespoon brown sugar, 1/2 cup stock/water, and 1.5 tablespoon cornstarch.

Curry Sauce

  1. 1
    Combine: 1.5 tablespoon curry paste, 1.5 tablespoon fish sauce, 2 teaspoon brown sugar, 1/2 cup stock/water, and 2 teaspoon cornstarch.

Orange-Ginger Sauce

  1. 1
    Combine: 1 inch ginger, 3 tablespoon soy sauce, 1 tablespoon rice vinegar, 1/2 cup orange juice (= 1 large orange), and 1.5 tablespoon cornstarch.

Black Pepper Sauce

  1. 1
    Combine: 3 tablespoon soy sauce, 2 teaspoon rice vinegar, 1 teaspoon brown sugar, 1/2 cup stock/water, 1.5 teaspoon black pepper, and 1 tablespoon cornstarch.

Sweet & Sour Sauce

  1. 1
    Combine: 1 tablespoon soy sauce, 2 tablespoon ketchup, 1 tablespoon rice vinegar, 1 tablespoon brown sugar, 2/3 cup stock/water, and 1.5 tablespoon cornstarch.

Protein

  1. 1
    Choose 1 pound worth of the following: Boneless skinless chicken thighs or breasts (cubed), Pork tenderloin (Cubed), Flank or Skirt Steak (sliced against the grain), Shrimp (defrosted, rinsed, and patted dry), Squid or calamari (sliced), Firm fish (like tilapia, mahi mahi, or salmon [cubed or sliced]), Extra-firm vacuum-packed tofu (cubed or sliced), Seitan (chopped), or Tempeh (chopped).

Aromatics

  1. 1
    Choose 1 tablespoon worth of the following: Garlic (minced), Shallots (minced or thinly sliced), Green onions (the white parts, chopped), or Ginger (minced or thinly sliced).

Veggies

  1. 1
    Choose 4 cups worth of the following: Onions (thinly sliced), Bell peppers (sliced), Carrots (thinly sliced), Celery (sliced), Chinese Eggplant (chopped), Bean Sprouts, Mushrooms (sliced), Broccoli/Cauliflower (chopped and microwaved with damp paper towel for 2 minutes), cabbage (Napa, green, red, or savoy, sliced), Baby spinach, Bok choy (chopped), Frozen peas, edamame, corn, Tomatoes (diced), Snow/sugar snap peas (trimmed and left whole), Green beans (trimmed), Kale (stems removed and leaves torn), Asparagus (ends trimmed and chopped), Zucchini (chopped), summer squash (chopped), or Baby corn (drained and rinsed).

Garnishes

  1. 1
    Chose any of fresh cilantro (chopped), Sesame seeds, Cashews or peanuts, Fresh basil (thinly sliced), Green onions (sliced or chopped, the greens), or Hot sauce.
